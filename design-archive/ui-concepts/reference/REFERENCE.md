# 10/10 reference set (Steam store screenshots, for study only)

What each one proves, mapped to GRADING-RUBRIC.md lines.

## HUD
- hud-01-battle-brothers: one bar at the bottom carries the whole army (portraits, health, morale); the field itself is almost clean. Rubric 2, 3, 7. Closest cousin to our group cards.
- hud-02-darkest-dungeon: grim-heroic feel with one hero panel; the hit number and blood splash read drama before any text. Rubric 7, 8. Note the single accent colour (red) on charcoal.
- hud-03-into-the-breach: thin top strip (power, grid), objectives in a corner, one tooltip at a time; nothing overlaps, everything aligns to a grid. Rubric 1, 4, 5, 10. The standard for "information budget".
- hud-04-kingdom-two-crowns: HUD almost invisible; coins and the world are the interface. Rubric 2, 3. Our "Marshal's Glass" ancestor.
- hud-05-hades: corners only, big action in the middle; health and ability tiles are small but unmistakable. Rubric 3, 7.

## Camp / menu UI
- ui-01-darkest-dungeon-roster: a dense sheet that still reads: three type sizes, one accent colour, every box aligned, portrait is the focal point. Rubric 4, 5, 8.
- ui-02-wartales-character: a sheet over the live world; the character is the focal point, stats in one quiet column, inventory tucked away. Rubric 3, 6.
- ui-03-slay-the-spire-cards: a card grid with coloured tabs; the cards carry only name, cost, one line; the rest is on hover. Rubric 2, 10. Direct analogue of our recruit screen.
- ui-04-battle-brothers-roster: parchment-and-iron dressing (our Iron Ledger / War Table cousins) without clutter: one panel, one portrait row, one action button. Rubric 8, 9, 10.

Common thread of every 10/10: one focal point, one accent colour, a visible grid, and far less text than you expect.
