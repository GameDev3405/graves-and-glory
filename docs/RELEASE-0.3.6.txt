Graves and Glory 0.3.6 — camp and friends local candidate
October 17, 2026

The camp is now a walled courtyard with four named stewards, recruitment and
equipment pavilions, contracts, a chapter monument and faction ready gates.
Camp displays gold and guidance without the battle scoreboard, tactical overlays,
combat orders, health bars or hotbar. R opens the camp guide. NPCs, tables and
menu shortcuts route to the same functions. Recruitment displays the remaining
pool and equal odds, confirms rewards from the server, and stops when complete.
Direct selection costs the same 100 gold as a random guaranteed-new unlock.

Friend parties are distinct from temporary private rooms. Up to four commanders
can invite, accept/decline, transfer leadership, leave, kick or disband. Invitations
expire after 60 seconds. Parties save with the host world and survive matches.
Private rooms have codes, host-selected maps, 1v1–4v4 capacity, bot fill, whole-party
team selection and individual ready checks. Results retain rooms for rematches.
Disconnecting clears readiness; idle rooms allow a two-minute reconnect grace.
Battle disconnects retain an army reservation and bots take over until return.
Parties persist across restarts; temporary rooms do not. Online commanders on the
same server can invite each other; this is not a cross-server friends directory.

Ready pads require a whole party on the same side, reject overcapacity, and
reset the countdown when the group changes. Step off to cancel. Camp guide map
votes count once per ready commander; changing a vote replaces it. Highest count
wins, ties use map order, and no votes retain the current map. A private room's
map preparation automatically starts its still-ready group when complete.

Progression begins with usable starter troops and a 100-gold tutorial reward.
The free, nonexpiring Founders chapter has 30 tiers at 250 XP each: 7,500 XP total.
Eligible matches award 100 XP plus 50 for victory. Three daily contracts grant
100 gold and 225 XP combined; one free reroll swaps the objective contract for
tactical orders. Weekly six-match completion grants 150 gold and 300 XP.
Daily resets use midnight UTC; weeks begin Monday UTC. Every fifth chapter tier
grants a title, ending in Bannerlord; other tiers grant 25 gold (600 total).
Titles give no combat advantage. Existing match gold remains 80 + 40 for victory,
or 40 in practice, with the existing three-minute eligibility requirement.

Progress and claims are server-owned during play. Unique match/claim identifiers
prevent repeated grants; atomic saves retain a previous-file backup. Failed claims
and recruitment saves roll back. Corrupt existing profiles are preserved and
reported rather than silently replaced with blank progress. Local/LAN collection
imports remain trusted; dedicated servers reject client collection imports.

Validation and remaining scope are recorded in the delivered verification report.
Local two-client tests cover invite, same-team entry, disconnect/reclaim, results,
rematch and persisted party readback. Rule tests cover capacity, expiration,
readiness, resets, repeated claims, clock rollback and all chapter rewards.

One battle runs per server. Passwords, spectators, simultaneous matches, a public
queue, cross-server accounts, full official-economy security and Internet hosting
remain future work. This candidate has not been installed into a personal launcher,
published, or tested as an eight-human Internet session.
