# Graves and Glory 1.0.0 - release notes (DRAFT)

Draft for Kort's wording. Nothing here has been released or published. `main` is still version 0.5.0 until the 1.0.0 candidate is recorded. Checked against `main` at 97bc7992. Things not yet in `main` are marked pending.

## What it is
Graves and Glory is a Fabric 1.20.1 mod for Minecraft: Java Edition. You collect troops, arrange a twenty-unit army, command it in real time and fight over objectives on large fantasy maps, solo or with friends. [Kort to confirm: tagline]

## Houses and troops
Five Houses, thirty troops: Gilded, Ironclad, Wildmoot, Tidecallers and Graveborn. Each House has Commons, Rares, Epics and Legendaries; the ten Commons are yours from the start, the rest come from rolls, chosen buys, Pacts and chapter rewards. In-match levels and gear tiers add depth, and the commander now fights in worn gear (next section). Troops draw their own models at one texel per pixel.
- Tier II and III art is on main for all five Houses (Gilded, Ironclad, Wildmoot, Tidecallers, Graveborn), with their own weapons.
- Each House now has its own look in the menus: marks, banners, braziers and card frames. Graveborn are violet and Tidecallers sea blue, and recruit banners match.

## Commander gear
The six commander classes and the H key are gone. Your commander fights in gear instead.
- Four sets, one per role: Last Watch (bruiser), Ashen Vigil (healer), Frostmarch Hunt (ranged) and Marchwarden (commander). There are 28 pieces in five rarities, from Common to Legendary, with set bonuses and perks. Weapons include swords, shields, greatswords, greataxes, bows, crossbows, quivers, healing staffs and war banners.
- What you wear sets your role, health, attack and armour. Gear works in battle through its stats, set bonuses and perks.
- Twelve relics give active abilities. You wear up to three and fire them with keys 1 to 3. The old class actives (Stand Fast, Bloodrush, Mark Prey, Rally Horn, Grave Bloom, Forward Supply) are now relics, and six new ones were added.
- The Arsenal is the gear screen. Press I in camp. It shows stats, what you wear and your relics, with filters, sorts, compare tooltips and equip. Breaking down a piece of Epic or better asks you to confirm.
- The Blacksmith stands near the Quartermaster and opens the Forge. At the Forge you roll for gear with marks, which you earn from settled matches. It has odds, pity, break down for duplicates, repair and more slots.
- Every new commander starts with a starter set for their old class. Saves move to schema 4 and older saves migrate.
- Pending: gear art. Worn gear shows as stand-in vanilla items until each piece has its own model.
- Pending: gear animations and gear sounds.
- Numbers are placeholders until the balance pass. [Kort to confirm]

## Sound
Combat, magic and music use royalty-free Pixabay recordings, with a music director that picks tracks for the moment. Sounds fire when the hit lands, the arrow leaves the bow or the event happens, not before. The bow release, arrow impact and armour ring were trimmed so they land on time. Pending: more cues (relics, the Forge, beasts, footsteps, ambience, music) are candidates in `work/audio-cues`, not installed yet. `tools/install_audio.py` installs them once Kort picks.

## Modes
- Siege: attackers storm a fortress gate stage by stage; defender archers man the towers (Ironhold).
- Convoy: escort a wagon along the road while the defence tries to stop it.
- Survival: hold against rising waves; allied bot banners count toward wave size. Feed, ledger and report lines are merged as draft text. [Kort to confirm: Survival is shipping in 1.0]
- Campaign: the six-battle Long March, solo only. Battle 1 teaches command, battles 2 and 3 are 2v2, battles 4 to 6 are 3v3. Each battle plays in its own mode and pays out, with victory gold. Difficulty climbs: battles 2 and 3 are Normal, 4 and 5 Hard, battle 6 hardest. Battle 6 is a Siege with allied bots from all five Houses. Battle 2's bonus is taking the gate down by 360 seconds. Briefings are drafts. [Kort to confirm: wording]
- Domination: hold points to 300; Practice and 1vAI remain.

## War Table, lobby and bots
The War Table is where you manage troops, gear, banner and Pact. Troop sheet, Armoury, Banner and Field pages, the campaign card and the lobby are all part of it, drawn at 3x scale. The six-seat lobby lets the host pick map and mode, with a 10-second countdown; troops are readied while you wait, so rounds start faster. Empty seats fill with Easy, Normal or Hard bots. A dropped player's seat is held for 2 minutes; a bot commands the army after 60 seconds. Interrupted matches pay a partial reward by time played.

## Performance
Server tick and frame-rate targets are met on the test machine. Round start was cut from about 105 ms to 29 ms cold by preparing troops in the lobby. Autosave is skipped during a battle.

## Install
- Modrinth .mrpack (Prism Launcher or any launcher that imports .mrpack): recommended. The pack install test passed.
- CurseForge zip: export and file-level check are merged; the mod fetch needs a CurseForge API key, so the CurseForge import is Kort's step.
- Plain zip, if you install Fabric Loader 0.16.14 yourself.
- Server kit: scripts for a dedicated server (port 25580, whitelist, backups, version gate). You set eula=true yourself.
Needs Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric API 0.92.2 or newer, Java 17+. All players must run the same build. [Kort to confirm: download links]

## Known issues
- Do not downgrade below 0.6 after playing with gear: older builds drop the gear fields from your commander save. A save from a newer build than the one you run is never overwritten; that session just does not save.
- No public matchmaking, accounts or paid service; friends host or use the server kit.
- Six-networked-player and Internet play are not independently verified. [Kort to confirm]
- Troop art is under review. Gear numbers are placeholders until the balance pass. [Kort to confirm]
