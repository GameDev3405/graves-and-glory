# Rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **No cheating or exploit abuse.** Report bugs in #bug-reports instead of exploiting them. Progression is not cheat-proof, so play fair.
3. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW content, gore beyond the game itself, or illegal material.
4. **No spam or self-promotion.** No unsolicited ads, invite links or mass pings.
5. **Use the right channel.** Bugs in #bug-reports, ideas in #suggestions, matches in #looking-for-match.
6. **No leaks or piracy.** Do not share pirated Minecraft or cracked accounts. Minecraft: Java Edition is required.
7. **Follow moderators.** Staff decisions are final in the moment. Appeal politely to an admin afterwards.
8. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.**

Breaking rules can lead to a warning, mute, kick or ban, depending on severity.
