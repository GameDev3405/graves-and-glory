# How to play

*DRAFT for Kort to confirm. Every key below is a default; change any of them under Options > Controls > Graves and Glory.*

**Controls**
- **R**: army commands (the order wheel)
- **G**: aim an attack order. **V**: follow me. **B**: cycle formation. **[ ]**: rotate the formation
- **Enter / Backspace**: confirm or cancel an order
- **M**: the battlefield map
- **Hold Left Alt**: show every key hint on the battle HUD
- **U**: the Armoury (your troops' kit). **N**: the Banner page of the War Table
- **I**: the Arsenal, your commander's gear (in camp only)
- **1, 2, 3**: fire your three relics in battle (arriving with the gear combat update)

**Your commander**
Your commander is a hero worth a few troops, not a solo army. What they do comes from their gear:
- **Armour**: helm, chest, legs and boots. Each piece belongs to one of four sets: bruiser, healer, ranged or commander.
- **Sets are your role.** Wear 2 or 4 pieces of one set to unlock its bonuses.
- **Weapons**: main hand and off hand, each suited to a role.
- **Relics**: three slots of active abilities with cooldowns. Any relic works in any build.

Everyone starts with a free Common set and weapon. Gear is never bought with money and can't be traded.

**The Blacksmith loop**
1. Fight a match. A win pays gold and about one roll's worth of **Forge marks**; a loss pays about half, and bot-only matches pay less.
2. In camp, visit the **Blacksmith**, 6 blocks south of the quartermaster, or press **I** and open the **Forge** tab.
3. Pick a slot and **roll**. Rarity runs Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and an Epic or better is guaranteed within 10 rolls.
4. In the **Arsenal** tab, click a piece to wear it, and hover it to compare against what you have on. Shift-click a piece you don't need to break it down for marks, or break down all duplicates at once.
5. Pay gold to repair worn gear (a broken piece gives half its stats) or to add a row to your inventory.

**Houses**
Your army is up to 20 troops drawn from five Houses, 30 troops in all. Build your banner on the War Table's Banner page, and kit troops in the Armoury.
