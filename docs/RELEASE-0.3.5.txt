Graves and Glory 0.3.5 — local candidate, October 17, 2026

Full-match arrivals remain in camp instead of being disconnected. Waiting
commanders are unseated: no extra army, match reward, capture contribution,
combat damage or battle introduction. R opens the war council while waiting.

camp boundary still returns them. Normal round completion brings everyone back.

Seat assignment counts seated armies only. New arrivals can take a bot seat
or an empty team slot without exceeding 1v1–4v4 capacity. Returning commanders
reclaim their reserved bot when available. Practice's opponent remains AI.
The existing fallback that replaces a reserved bot when no other bot is free
is preserved outside practice; durable reconnect reservations remain future work.

New server templates allow 16 connections to leave camp space beside a full
8-commander match. Existing servers need their max-players setting updated.
This does not establish a measured 16-player hosting/performance guarantee.
