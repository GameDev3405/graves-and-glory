Graves and Glory 1.0.0 — release candidate (draft)
November 4, 2026

Graves and Glory is a Fabric mod for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.20.1. You
collect troops, arrange an army of twenty, command it in real time and fight
over objectives on large fantasy maps, alone or with friends.

Five Houses, thirty troops: the Gilded Order, Ironclad, Wildmoot, Tidecallers
and Graveborn. The ten Commons are yours from the start; the rest come from
rolls, chosen buys, Pacts and chapter rewards. Tier II and III troop art is in
for all five Houses, and each House has its own look in the menus. Graveborn
are violet and Tidecallers sea blue.

Your commander now fights in gear instead of a class. There are four sets
(Last Watch, Ashen Vigil, Frostmarch Hunt and Marchwarden) and 28 pieces from
Common to Legendary. What you wear sets your role, health, attack and armour.
Twelve relics give active abilities, three at a time, on keys 1 to 3. Press I
in camp for the Arsenal; the Blacksmith's Forge rolls, breaks down and repairs
gear. Every new commander starts with a starter set. Saves move to schema 4,
and older saves migrate.

The War Table holds the troop sheet, Armoury, Banner and Field pages and the
campaign card. The six-seat lobby lets the host pick the map and mode and
starts a 10-second countdown. Empty seats fill with Easy, Normal or Hard bots.
A dropped player's seat is held for 2 minutes, then a bot commands the army.
An interrupted match pays a partial reward by time played.

Modes: Domination, Siege, Convoy, Survival and the six-battle Long March
campaign (solo). Practice and 1vAI remain. Ironhold is the adopted battle map.

Combat, magic and music now use royalty-free recordings, with a music director
that picks tracks for the moment. Sounds fire when the hit lands. Round start
is faster because troops are prepared while you wait in the lobby.

Install: import the .mrpack into Prism Launcher or any launcher that supports
it, or use the plain zip with Fabric Loader 0.16.14 yourself. Needs Minecraft
1.20.1, Java 17 or newer and Fabric API 0.92.2 or newer. Everyone in a game
must run the same build. A server kit is available for a dedicated server; you
supply the EULA agreement yourself.

Not in this build: gear art (worn gear shows as stand-in vanilla items), gear
animations and gear sounds, and more
battle maps. Gear numbers are placeholders until the balance pass. There is no
public matchmaking, no account system and no paid service. Six-player and
Internet play have not been independently verified. Do not go back to a build
older than 0.6 after playing with gear: older builds drop the gear fields from
your commander save.
