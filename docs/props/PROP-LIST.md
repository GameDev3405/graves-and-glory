Draft for Kort, 2026-11-09. Chapter 1 follows the lore draft `docs/drafts/chapter-1-outline-2026-11-09.md` (awaiting Kort);
map layouts are Kort's, so this lists pieces builders can place, not where. Builder: **S** = Sonnet 5.5 high (routine),
**O** = Opus 5.5 medium (flagship or story piece). Size is cells x*y*z.
