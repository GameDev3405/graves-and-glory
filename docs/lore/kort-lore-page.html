Graves and Glory: the world, chapter one and the seasons

The land is the bodies of sleeping giants. Their thrown-away magic is in every troop you field. Five Houses fight over old wrongs while the ground starts to wake.

1 / The world in one screen

Wakeland

The giants

Ages ago the earthen giants grew bored of living, built a spire to drain their own mana, and crumbled into mountains, plains and sea floor. Five elders lie under Wakeland. The leader, Orruvan, lies under Highheart, the Gilded capital.

The spire has woken. The giants want every drop of glimpur destroyed so it can never do harm again. They are calm, courteous, and right about the harm. For four Houses the cure is extinction. They threw the mana away themselves.

Glimpur

The giants' leaked mana, a violet-white shimmer. The giants call it meru, their breath. Sunweavers cast it, Wildmoot grew cunning on it, Ironclad graft it as redstone, Tidecallers were remade by it, and the Graveborn were bound by it.

The Skeleton King

Halvard of Tengervald, called the Holdfast: a human king who bound the dead to save a realm the confederacy abandoned. His binding tripped the spire. He survives two acts; the giants execute him in the third.

Three acts

ActSeasonsWhat happens
I. The Ring1 to 5One House chapter per season. Giants only hinted. Ends on the spire's light.
II. The Waking6 to 8The leader elder wakes under the capital. Gilded offered terms. The Houses split.
III. The Choice9 to 11All elders wake, execute the Skeleton King, then break. The weary leader chooses to live. Giants become playable.
After12 onDemacrus, new peoples, other giant bodies.

One season is one month. Chapter 1 ships in 1.0; chapter N arrives in season N.

Rules for a new unit

One House and its species. Its magic is glimpur used the House's way. It has a part in the House's grievance. It keeps the House's look; violet belongs to the giants. No giants or giant technology before Act II. No new named characters without you. Stats stay with balance.

Rules for a new House or faction

Written before any art: which giant's body it lives on, how glimpur made it, one wrong done to it and one it did, its myth of the giants, a voice, a Legendary, its season and act, and what the cure means for it. Only humans are ever offered terms.

Open hooks for years of seasons: the Charter never says who is a person; Tengervald's living people; Gilded who take the terms; Ironclad refused as kin; survivors of the drowned towns; the binding's heir; elders beyond the sea; Demacrus; shared glimpur after Act III.

4 / Named, as you asked

The names and what they mean

Humans name things for what they see and are usually wrong about what lies underneath. The giants name things for what they are, from a few roots, so a player who learns the roots can read every giant name.

NameWhat it isMeaning
Wakelandthe landNamed by the settlers for the wake their ships cut across the sea. A wake is also the watch kept over the dead. And the land is about to wake. True three ways; they meant only the first.
Highheartthe capitalBuilt on high ground because the stone there was always warm. It sits on Orruvan's heart, over the spire.
the Concordthe human confederacy"Of one heart", bound by the Charter. It never asked whose hearts counted.
Merudaalthe spire"The Last Breath" (meru breath, daal last). The giants breathed their power out through it. No human knows the name until Act II.

5 / What you decided, and what is next

Done and next

  • One chapter per one-month season. The story parts are now Acts I to III: Act I is seasons 1 to 5, the giants wake in season 6.
  • Chapter 1 approved. You field your own troops in 1.0, beside Gilded banners. A fixed House army per chapter has to be ready for chapter 2, one month after launch, so it is built with the 1.0 campaign code.
  • All chapter 1 code approved. A build brief is ready for the coordinator to hand to a builder: choosing enemies per battle, after-battle lines, the magic flicker, removing "The Long March", resetting old campaign progress while keeping Pacts and Legendaries, plus season locks.
  • New maps only where the story needs them (seasons 4, 5, 6), so your map time stays manageable at one season a month.
  • The Bound Host arrives as an enemy faction in season 5, the giants in season 6.
  • Season locks, approved: each chapter unlocks when its season starts, earlier chapters stay open forever, and a new player starts at chapter 1.
