Graves and Glory: the world, chapter one and the seasons

The land is the bodies of sleeping giants. Their thrown-away magic is in every troop you field. Five Houses fight over old wrongs while the ground starts to wake.

1 / The world in one screen

Eldmark

The giants

Ages ago the earthen giants grew bored of living, built a spire to drain their own mana, and crumbled into mountains, plains and sea floor. Five elders lie under Eldmark, the leader under the Gilded capital.

The spire has woken. The giants want every drop of glimpur destroyed so it can never do harm again. They are calm, courteous, and right about the harm. For four Houses the cure is extinction. They threw the mana away themselves.

Glimpur

The giants' leaked mana, a violet-white shimmer. Sunweavers cast it, Wildmoot grew cunning on it, Ironclad graft it as redstone, Tidecallers were remade by it, and the Graveborn were bound by it.

The Skeleton King

A human king of Tengervald who bound the dead to save a realm the confederacy abandoned. His binding tripped the spire. He survives two acts; the giants execute him in the third.

Three acts

Rules for a new unit

One House and its species. Its magic is glimpur used the House's way. It has a part in the House's grievance. It keeps the House's look; violet belongs to the giants. No giants or giant technology before Act II. No new named characters without you. Stats stay with balance.

Rules for a new House or faction

Written before any art: which giant's body it lives on, how glimpur made it, one wrong done to it and one it did, its myth of the giants, a voice, a Legendary, its season and act, and what the cure means for it. Only humans are ever offered terms.

Open hooks for years of seasons: the Charter never says who is a person; Tengervald's living people; Gilded who take the terms; Ironclad refused as kin; survivors of the drowned towns; the binding's heir; elders beyond the sea; Demacrus; shared glimpur after Act III.

4 / Your answers needed

Decisions for Kort

  1. Pace. One chapter per season, so Act I runs seasons 1 to 5? Or two chapters per season, Act I over three seasons? Recommend one per season.
  2. Acts. The approved design called its three story parts "seasons". Rename them Acts I to III so they do not clash with server seasons? Recommend yes.
  3. Season length. How long is a server season? It sets how slowly the giants arrive.
  4. Whose troops in chapter 1. Today the campaign fields your own collection banner, any House. Keep that in 1.0 with Gilded banners beside you, or build a fixed Gilded banner for the chapter? Recommend your own banner in 1.0, a fixed House banner before season 2.
  5. New code for chapter 1. Required: choose the enemy House per battle (today enemies come from difficulty rows, so the briefings name the wrong enemies and a hard battle would field the Hollow King), and retire "The Long March" from player text. Required for the lore seeds you chose: a line after each battle, and the magic flicker on casters. Also a plan change: the 1.0 plan says no named cast, and the after lines let Aldric speak. Approve?
  6. Chapter 1 as drafted. The six battles, their names and lines, including the Charter line "every wrong is answered" and the placeholder name Tengervald in player text.
  7. Your maps. A carved stone at a Kingsfield quarry cut, a stone hand on Rivergate, a statue on Ironhold, when you design them?
  8. Old progress. The new battles need new ids, or old profiles would show them as cleared with the Pact already paid. Reset chapter 1 for everyone, or count old clears as new ones? This is a save change.
  9. New enemy factions. The Skeleton King's Bound Host as an enemy faction in season 5, and the giants as an enemy in season 6?
  10. Still unnamed. Eldmark, the Skeleton King, the spire, the capital, the confederacy, and the five elder names (Orruvan, Kharuun, Ysmorren, Ommeru, Thalluun). Keep the placeholders or rename?
