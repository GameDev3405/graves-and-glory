# Names of the world

Status: chosen by the lore lead at Kort's request, 2026-11-09 ("be meaningful with the names"). They replace every placeholder in the design, the bible and the outlines. Kort may veto any one. Names Kort gave himself (Demacrus, Thruggsen, Tengervald, glimpur and the shipped House, troop and map names) stay.

Two languages carry the names. Humans name things for what they see and want, and are usually wrong about what lies under them. The giants name things for what they are, slowly, in long vowels built from a few roots, so a player who learns the roots can read every giant name.

## The giants' roots

| Root | Meaning |
|---|---|
| orru | enough, finished |
| van | counted, measured |
| meru | breath; also the giants' word for glimpur |
| om | wide |
| daal | last |
| khar | fire |
| uun | heart, core |
| ys | quiet |
| morren | under snow |
| thal | deep |
| luun | mother, the one who holds |

Glimpur is the human word. The giants call it **meru**, their breath. Every House's magic is the giants' last breath, breathed out on purpose.

## Places and powers

| Thing | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| The land | **Wakeland** | The settlers from Demacrus named it for the long wake their ships cut across the sea. In their old speech a wake is also the watch kept over the dead before burial. The land is a body, and it is about to wake. The name is true three ways and the people who chose it meant only the first. |
| The spire | **Merudaal**, "the Last Breath" | The giants built it to breathe their meru out for good. Humans have never seen it and have no name for it; the player first hears this one from a giant in Act II. |
| The capital | **Highheart** | The founders built on the high ground because the stone there was always warm. The city stands on Orruvan's chest, over its heart and over the Last Breath. |
| The confederacy | **the Concord** | Concord means "of one heart": the realms bound together under the Charter. The Concord never asked whose hearts counted, which is the Charter's gap. |
| The Charter | **the Concord Charter** | Unchanged in use: "the Charter" in speech. |

Ommeru and Merudaal share a root on purpose: the plains wind and the spire are both the giants' breath.

## The Skeleton King

**Halvard of Tengervald, called the Holdfast.** Halvard is an old mountain name meaning "guardian of the rock". In life his people called him the Holdfast because he would not let a pass fall or a village go. When the Concord struck Tengervald from its relief list he held on past death, his people's and his own. The other Houses call him the Skeleton King. The name works three ways: he guarded his realm; he guarded it standing on a giant's body without knowing; and "holdfast" is the thing that will not let go, which is what the binding is.

The rebels' leader stays the Hollow King, the mocking title the freed dead gave Halvard's defected general.
