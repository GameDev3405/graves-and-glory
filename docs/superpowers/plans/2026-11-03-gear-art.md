# Commander gear art (part f) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make part (f): the hand-authored art for commander gear: 4 sets x 4 pieces x 5 rarity looks, the weapon families, 12 relic icons, the grid icons, the commander render on the screen and in battle, and the greatweapon, staff and banner clips.

**Architecture:** Every gear piece is a small hand-authored model built with the troop hand library (`art/hand/common/handlib.py`: `Box`, `Bone`, `pack`, `paint_box`, ramps and painters) and rendered offline by `art/hand/common/render.py`, so every batch is reviewed as a contact sheet before any game code exists. A new `art/hand/gear/gearlib.py` adds a `GearPiece` frame: bones parented to the six biped bones, one skin sheet per rarity (rarity 1 = Common to 5 = Legendary, the `gearRarities` order), one cloth sheet per side, an optional glow sheet, and an install that writes `models/gear/<item>.json` plus its textures. The game side comes last: a client feature renderer draws the worn pieces on the player in place of the vanilla stand-in armour, and a player-model hook plays the new commander clips.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 + Pillow (art builders, offline renderer, unittest checks); Java 17 Fabric client code (renderer, clips) after parts (c) and (d) merge; the real-client harness for in-game checks.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-commander-gear-design.md` (Art and Animation sections). Catalogue: `docs/drafts/gear-catalogue-2026-11-03.md` (approved by Kort 2026-11-03). Index row 5: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-commander-gear-index.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Souls-like game art, not historical or Minecraft-mod looks. Every piece is unique (ART-STYLE-GUIDE 10.0 rule 4; memory `kort-weapon-taste`).
- Strictly 1 texel per model pixel, hand-authored in the style of `art/hand/knight/build_knight.py` (10.0 rule 5). 6- and 7-tone hue-shifted ramps as in handlib.
- No flag, pennant or standard on any back (10.0 rule 1; `no-back-flags`). Other back gear (quiver, pauldron backs, cowl) is fine. The war banners are held in the hand only.
- Cloth parts (tabard, sash, hood cloth, faulds edge, banner cloth) are drawn from the side's cloth sheet; metal and leather are fixed paint. Gear is house-neutral: no House accent ramps (`OCHRE`, `SEA`, Graveborn violet) in gear paint.
- The weapon always points the logical way for the attack; check every keyframe (`weapon-faces-the-move`).
- Rarity looks follow the catalogue per set (Common bare, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple plus glow, Legendary orange light plus a changed silhouette). The rarity colours themselves are the sheet borders: Common `#9d9d9d`, Uncommon `#3fae49`, Rare `#3d7fd6`, Epic `#9a4fd6`, Legendary `#e8892b`.
- Item and relic ids are the ones in `sheets/design.json` on `feat/gear-foundation` (commit `ef971caf`); never invent ids. The art builds against a pinned copy until that branch merges.
- At most 2 Minecraft instances; drive clients through the harness, never desktop computer-use. Never touch PIDs 29360, 4580, 6048, 17380, ports 25565-25567 and 25571, or `C:\GGBuild`. Never hand-edit `Spec.java`.
- No push, no publish, no merge to `main`. Every visual batch goes to Kort through the coordinator session as a contact sheet; nothing is final without his visual approval. Questions go through the coordinator.
- Rubric: player-facing art, threshold 9; observable findings drive corrections; at most two automatic correction rounds per batch.

## Review Focus

1. **Side colour on every side.** A cloth part that reads well on crimson may vanish on `company` navy or `linen`. Each set's contact sheet shows the Legendary on crimson and teal, and a check renders all six sides without an exception (Task 2, `test_every_side_builds`).
2. **The player's own skin under the gear.** Pieces cover only their slot; a commander wearing two sets, or nothing in one slot, must still look whole. The mannequin contact sheet includes one mixed loadout (Task 3 Step 2), and the renderer test wears one piece alone (Task 9).
3. **Overlap between slots.** Chest pauldrons over helm cowls, greaves under a skirt, boots inside leggings: boxes of different slots must not z-fight. A check fails when two slots' boxes share a face plane over the same area (Task 1, `test_no_coplanar_faces_between_slots`).
4. **Rarity reads at battle distance.** Legendary must differ in silhouette, not only colour; Epic must differ from Rare without the glow. A check counts silhouette pixels (Task 1, `test_legendary_silhouette_grows`) and the contact sheet adds a 2 px-per-texel far row.
5. **Back of the commander.** The back view is where a banner or pennant could sneak in; a check refuses any bone tagged or named `flag`, `pennant`, `standard` or `banner` outside a held weapon (Task 1, `test_no_back_flags`).

## Decisions logged (reversible; Kort can overturn at review)

- **D1 Base body.** Gear is drawn over the player's own skin; uncovered slots show it. The mannequin stands in for it on contact sheets.
- **D2 Rarity count per piece.** Each piece has five skin sheets, not a base plus overlays, so each rarity can change shape (Legendary adds bones: `stages=[5]` on `Bone`).
- **D3 Icons.** Grid icons are 32 x 32 sprites drawn 1:1 on the 960 x 540 canvas inside the 44 px slots. They start from an offline 3/4 render of the piece at 2 px per texel, then are hand-cleaned per rarity; relic icons are painted from ASCII grids.
- **D4 Weapon list.** The art follows the sheet's 10 weapon items, not the catalogue's 11: the sheet adds Notched Falchion (`falchion`) and lacks Sunwell Crook and The Long Colours. Queued as a question for Kort (Q-art-1); the extra two are built only if he wants them in the sheet.
- **D5 Families.** The spec's 5 weapon families are sword (with the shield), greatweapon (greatsword, great-axe), bow and crossbow (with the quiver), staff, and banner.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Gear frame, ids and checks

**Files:**
- Create: `art/hand/gear/ids.json`, `art/hand/gear/gearlib.py`, `art/hand/gear/mannequin.py`, `art/hand/gear/build_gear.py`
- Test: `test-tools/standalone/test_gear_art.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `handlib.Box`, `Bone`, `pack`, `paint_box`, `SIDES`, `TEAM`, ramps; `render.render`, `render.sheet_of`.
- Produces:
  - `RARITIES = (1, 2, 3, 4, 5)`; `RARITY_NAMES = ("common", "uncommon", "rare", "epic", "legendary")`.
  - `class GearPiece(item_id, slot, skin=(64, 64), cloth=(64, 32), bones=callable, cloth_painter=callable, glow=None)`; `bones()` returns `Bone`s whose top parents are `head`, `body`, `right_arm`, `left_arm`, `right_leg` or `left_leg`; `build() -> (bones, {rarity: skin}, {side: cloth}, glow_or_None)`; `outputs() -> {asset path: Image or JSON}`.
  - `model_json(piece)` format: `{"format": 1, "item": id, "slot": slot, "density": 1, "size": [w, h], "cloth": "<base>team_cloth_", "skins": {"1".."5": path}, "glow": path or "", "glowFrom": 4, "bones": [same bone rows as handlib's troop_json]}`.
  - `mannequin.bones()` and `mannequin.skin()`: the biped with handlib bone names.
  - `contact(pieces, out, rarities=RARITIES, sides=("crimson", "teal"), px=6)`: one row per rarity, the pieces worn together on the mannequin, views front, 3/4 front, side, back, 3/4 back, and a far column at px 2.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write `test_gear_art.py`** with, per set module that exists:
  - `test_ids_known`: every `GearPiece.item_id` is in `ids.json`; when `sheets/design.json` has `gearItems`, `ids.json` matches it (id, slot, set).
  - `test_one_texel_per_pixel`: every box's painted face is exactly its size in texels (each `Box.uv` footprint from `pack` equals `w x h`, `d x h`, `w x d` with integer sizes), and the JSON's `density` is 1.
  - `test_every_rarity_paints`: each rarity skin has opaque texels under every box shown at that rarity.
  - `test_every_side_builds`: cloth sheets build for all six `SIDES`; a team bone has only `sheet="cloth"` boxes and a non-team bone none.
  - `test_no_back_flags`: no bone name or tag contains `flag`, `pennant`, `standard` or `banner` in any armour piece.
  - `test_no_coplanar_faces_between_slots`: for every pair of pieces of different slots in a set, no two boxes share a face plane over an overlapping area.
  - `test_legendary_silhouette_grows`: the Legendary's front and side silhouettes on the mannequin cover at least 6% more pixels than the Common's.
  - `test_house_neutral`: no skin texel uses a colour from handlib's House accent ramps (`wildmoot.OCHRE`, `tidecallers.SEA`, `graveborn.VIOLET`), so the Epic purple uses its own ramp.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run it:** `python -m unittest test-tools/standalone/test_gear_art.py -v`. Expected: fails on the missing `gearlib`.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `ids.json`, `gearlib.py`, `mannequin.py` and `build_gear.py`** with the interfaces above; `ids.json` is generated once by `git show ef971caf:sheets/design.json` filtered to the listed fields.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the tests.** Expected: pass with no set modules (set-dependent cases skip).
