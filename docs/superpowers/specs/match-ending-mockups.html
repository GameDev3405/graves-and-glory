The camera cuts to one shared stage that stands outside every map, so the map redesigns never touch it. Each commander stands in their own gear with three of their troops, in their side's colours. Both teams can be on it, bots too. With fewer than three commanders the empty plinths stay bare.

Ranked by a match score: 10 per troop killed, 30 per enemy commander you downed, 1 per second on the objective, and up to 100 for troops kept alive. One minute holding a point is worth six kills, so objective players rank fairly. The weights can be tuned in the design sheet.

Calls I made (yours to change)

  • Step lengths: 6, 8, 10 and 6 seconds (30 in total). The rewards step stretches a little when a campaign win has many lines.
  • Skip uses the same keys as skipping the entrance. Skipping only moves you; skipping the rewards sends you straight back to the room.
  • The match score weights above, and the MVP tie-break: more kills, then more objective time, then the winning side.
  • The stage stands near the camp, outside every map: dark stone, three oak plinths, the MVP's side banners, a slow camera push-in.
  • Each commander's three troops are their troop types with the most kills; someone with no kills shows their banner's first types.
  • Troop animations pause at the freeze (statues), not settle into idle.
  • This replaces the 10 November plan for the match end (8 s "Ended" plus a 4 s best-commander orbit). Play again and surrender from that plan are not part of this.
