# No routing: troops do what they are told

Status: **approved by Kort as written, 2026-10-22** (after the builder check and his no-horns ruling). Kort's answers from the brainstorm are marked **(Kort)**. Calls the
designer made where Kort did not rule are marked **(designer's call)**; Kort may overturn any of them. Nothing here is
built.

This design removes routing and morale from the game, for every side, and replaces each job routing did. It changes
parts of six approved specs (Houses, unit identity, unit AI, Phase 4 modes, UI, campaign story), Kort's vision and the
approved in-match progression design, and it rewrites code already built on `phase-2-houses` (Phase 1's morale and
Phase 2 Tasks 6 to 8). Section 12 lists every change to a document, section 11 every change to built code, section 13
every plan change. This design edits none of them itself.

Sources: Kort's direction of 2026-10-21 (below), his click-through answers in this brainstorm, `sheets/design.json` on
`phase-1-solo-core` and `phase-2-houses` (rows `morale`, `botCommand`, `counters`, `houseTraits`, `abilities`,
`classes`, `troops`), the code on `phase-2-houses` at 0f068e3 and b5f65a3 (`Morale`, `BattleMath`, `CounterMath`, `Houses`,
`Commander`, `Abilities`, `Campaign.botDecide`, `GravesAndGlory`, `CommandMode`, `Phase1Checks`, `Phase2Checks`), the
HUD code on `ui-plan` (`BattleHud`, `FeedLines`, `FeedEvents`), `docs/PHASE-2-BALANCE.md` on `phase-2-houses`,
`docs/copy/chapter-1.md`, and the specs and plans named in sections 12 and 13.

Kort's direction (2026-10-21, his words): "i actually want to remove routing, if i send my troops somewhere they stay
there and continue the job without question, they dont get a choice or have a moral or anything. they just do what
they are told, this is a big change so really think about that."

## 1. Intent

A troop goes where it is sent and does the job until it dies or its commander gives a new order. Nothing in the game
takes that decision away from the commander: no morale, no break, no automatic run for home. This extends Kort's
earlier unit AI ruling ("hold means never break", 2026-10-18) from Hold to every order and every side.

Why this is better for the game:
- Orders mean what they say. The player's skill is choosing where to fight and when to pull out, not managing a
  hidden morale number (unit AI goal: obedience, zero violations).
- Fights are decided by what the player can see: bodies falling, strength bars, flanks turned, veterans' chevrons
  (pillar 1, readable drama).
- One rule for everyone. Bots get no hidden advantage or penalty from morale; a bot commander pulls back only by
  giving the same Retreat order the player has (unit AI anti-goal: a bot order the player cannot give).

### Goals
1. No troop of any side ever leaves its order unasked. **(Kort)** The one thing a troop still does unasked is what the
   unit AI spec already allows: shields rise and menders heal.
2. Bot commanders may still order a retreat or fall back; that is a choice, made with the player's own orders.
   **(Kort, earlier click-through)**
3. Every job routing did is replaced on purpose: the flank payoff (section 4), the readable drama (section 5), the way
   a beaten group leaves a fight (section 6), and each House and class ability built on it (section 7).
4. Each replacement keeps its House's identity and copies no sibling's signature ability (unit identity rule).
5. Every command and every ability is given from the screen (the command list, its key rows and the ability slot),
   never by using an item, a weapon or a horn. Horns are not heard in the field; a command the player clicks may play
   a horn sound to him as feedback. **(Kort, 2026-10-21, during this design's review)**

### Non-goals
- No new orders. Retreat exists today; the Regroup order belongs to the supply-house design
  (`2026-10-22-supply-houses-design.md`) and lands with it **(Kort)**.
- No change to the bots' choice of when to retreat (`botCommand.retreatShare`, `rallyShare`, and later the unit AI
  doctrines' `fallBackShare`, `rallyShare`). These are order thresholds, not breaks.
- No new House traits, troops or classes. Every change is to an existing row.
- Combat numbers outside the rows named here stay as they are.

### Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)
- A morale system under another name: any rule that makes a troop stop, slow, run or fight worse because its group
  is losing. (A troop may fight *better* when wounded; that is an ability, not a break. Section 7.4.)
- A Hold line that can only be beaten by grinding it down head-on, so flanking stops mattering.
- A group that dies to the last man because the player could not tell it was losing.
- Two Houses whose replacements do the same thing.

## 3. The rule

**No group of any side ever breaks.** **(Kort)** The whole of Phase 1's rout behaviour goes:
- no strength or flank test every 20 ticks, no `RoutGuard`, no rout shares;
- no automatic Retreat assignment to the base, no dropped targets, no rally, no regroup immunity (`steadyUntil`);
- no morale number anywhere: not on group cards, not in the state packet, not in the end-of-match report. The card's
  strength bar stays; it is health, not morale.

A group keeps its order, its formation and its fight until every member is dead or its commander gives a new order.
Reinforcements join their group and take its current order, as today. Temporary troops (Grave Bloom's Risen) follow
their group's orders, as today.

This applies in every mode: Practice, Skirmish, Campaign, multiplayer and the Phase 4 modes (section 9)
**(designer's call, following Kort's "everyone")**.

## 4. Flanking

Today a group struck by three or more enemies from outside its facing arc breaks at 60% strength instead of 25%
(`morale.flankedRoutShare`). That was the main payoff for going around a line. Counter row C16 already gives melee
blows from the flank or rear x1.15, measured against the struck troop's own facing (`CounterMath`, condition `flank`,
bearing over 110 degrees).

### 4.1 Harder blows **(Kort)**

| Row | Attacker | Target | Condition | Today | New |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C16 | `melee` | any | `flank` (bearing over the flank arc) | x1.15 | **x1.30** |
| C16s (new) | `shot` (arrow, bolt) | any | `flank` | none | **x1.15** |

- The numbers are starting values; the sims tune them (section 10).
- Shields still cover the front only (C1 to C3 are `raised_front` and `lowered_front`), so a struck troop that turns
  to face the attacker behind it exposes its back to the enemy in front. That stays as built.
- Area damage (blight, shell, mist, and the splash of a slam or cleave on the others around the target) gets no flank
  bonus: it has no direction. The blow on the struck target itself is a melee hit and takes C16 by its bearing
  **(designer's call; today a slam's splash carries the `melee` tag and takes C16)**.
- Wildmoot's *Pack Hunters* keeps its own rear bonus (+10/20/30%) and stacks on top of C16, so Wildmoot stays the best
  flankers **(designer's call)**.
- The flank arc (110 degrees) moves from the `morale` sheet to `combatDiscipline` (section 8); C16, C16s and Pack
  Hunters keep reading one shared number.

### 4.2 Covered flanks (Gilded Bulwark) **(Kort)**

Today Bulwark has two halves: a ranged-damage cut for Gilded shield troops, and a rout guard for any own group whose
centre is within R (5/8/12 by tier) of a living Gilded troop outside that group. The ranged cut stays as built. The
rout guard becomes **covered flanks**:

- A troop of the army is **covered** while a living Gilded troop of the same army, from a different group, stands
  within the tier's radius R of it (5, 8 or 12 blocks, the radii of today's rows).
- C16 and C16s do not apply to a covered target. Every other row still does, Pack Hunters included **(designer's
  call: Wildmoot's own trait still beats the Gilded anchor, which keeps a counter to it)**.
- "From a different group" mirrors today's guard ("a group never holds itself") **(designer's call)**: Gilded archers
  cover the infantry line in front of them, and Gilded knights cover the archers behind them. A Gilded infantry group
  cut off from the rest of its army can still be flanked.
- Measured from the struck troop's position, not its group's centre, because the rule now acts per blow
  **(designer's call)**.

This keeps the Gilded as the anchor of a mixed line, which is what "troops near a Gilded unit do not rout" gave them.

## 5. Reading the fight

### 5.1 LAST STAND **(Kort)**

A group of the player's army whose strength (living health over the health it fielded, `BattleMath.strength`, as
today) is at or below `lastStandShare` (0.25, today's `routShare`) while an enemy troop or commander stands within
`lastStandRadius` (16, today's `engageCheckRadius`) of its centre is **at its last stand**. The troops do nothing
different; it is information for the commander, who decides whether to pull them out.

- **Group card:** the slot the ROUTING state used. The card keeps its live look (it is still fighting: no grey card,
  no faded glyphs), carries the stamp and the words LAST STAND, and its count turns red. A FALLEN group keeps today's
  grey card. Card art and layout are the builder's within the approved Iron Ledger family; graded 9 on the rubric.
  Order of card states, first match wins: FALLEN, LAST STAND, FLASH, SELECTED, PLAIN.
- **Feed:** one line to the owner when a group turns to its last stand: "Infantry make a last stand" (the group label,
  as the rout line used). At most one line per group per 30 s (`lastStandFeedSeconds`) **(designer's call: a group
  hovering at the line must not spam the feed)**. Glyph: the stamp; ink: today's grey #504b44 rout ink.
- **No sound** for a last stand **(designer's call: sounds now answer only the player's own commands, section 5.2;
  Kort may ask for a cue)**.
- The state is evaluated every 20 ticks, where `Morale.tick` ran.
- The enemy's last stands are not shown (cards show only the player's own army), as with routs today.
- It covers the groups that have cards today: infantry, ranged and support (`Morale.GROUPS`, the three-group state
  packet). Cavalry and siege troops have no card and never had morale; they get LAST STAND, by the same rule, when the
  parked five-group card panel lands (`docs/PARKED.md`, the Task 8 ruling) **(designer's call)**.

### 5.2 Commands come from the screen; no horns in the field **(Kort)**

Kort, 2026-10-21: "i want horns removed. i want all commands and tasks assigned via screen, not vias weapons or horns
etc. if a player clicks retreat from his commands list it can play a horn sound but i do not want the use of actual
items."

What the code does today: no command is given through an item. Orders come from the R council and the selected
card's key row; the class ability comes from the H key and the ability slot beside the hotbar. The commander carries
a sword and a bow to fight, never to command. Two things make it look otherwise, and both change:
- **The ability slot shows a horn picture for every ability** (a Phase 1 placeholder, `hud/horn`). It shows each
  ability's own picture instead, drawn as a HUD slot that cannot be mistaken for a hotbar item **(designer's call)**.
- **Horns sound in the world** on events: a charge (any side, bots included), a rout, a rally, the Oath Banner plant,
  the Marshal's Rally Horn. All of these go.

The rule from now on:
- Every command and ability is given from the screen: the command list (R council), its key rows and the ability
  slot. Keys are shortcuts for those screen entries, not items **(designer's call)**. No item, weapon or placed block
  ever issues an order, and no future design adds one.
- **No horn is heard in the field.** Bots, troops and abilities make no horn sound: not on a charge, a retreat, a last
  stand or a banner plant.
- **Command feedback:** when the player gives a command from his screen, it may play a short sound to him alone, as
  UI feedback **(Kort, for Retreat; designer's call for the rest)**:

| Command the player gives | Feedback sound (to him only) |
|---|---|
| Retreat (and later Fall back and Regroup) | the retreat horn (`horn_rout`) |
| Charge | the charge horn (`horn_charge`) |
| Muster (the Marshal's ability, 7.3) | the rally horn (`horn_rally`) |
| every other order | the existing UI click, as today |

  At most one feedback sound of a kind per `hornCooldownSeconds` (8). The sound ids may keep their names, as `writ`
  did.
- The title screen's horn drawing and its hover sound stay: they are menu art, not a command **(designer's call;
  Kort may cut them)**.

Readable drama without horns: a charge reads as a group breaking into a run, a retreat as a group turning away, a
last stand as the card stamp (5.1). The unit AI's tells that were horns (T1 `horn_charge`, T2 `horn_commit`) need
cues that are not horns (section 12.4).

### 5.3 Colour-blind and rubric

The colour-blind rule 2 of the UI spec reads "last stand the stamp and the words LAST STAND". Rubric items 6 and 7
name "last stand" where they named "rout" (section 12.6).

## 6. Pulling a beaten group out

The player's own orders replace the automatic rout **(Kort)**:
- **Retreat** (exists): the group disengages to the aimed spot at x1.15 speed and fights nothing on the way, even
  when struck. Reinforcements join it and it fills back up, as today.
- **Fall back** (unit AI, not built): a fighting withdrawal at 0.8 pace.
- **Regroup** (supply-house design): the group marches to a held supply house or the base, holds in its yard and
  heals, its new troops deploy into it, then it waits for orders.

**Arrival (designer's call, from the builder check).** Built today, Retreat and Move never end: both have `engage`
false, nothing converts them on arrival, and only the routing rally ever ended a Retreat. Without routing that would
leave a pulled-out group standing passive, even when struck, until it is ordered again, and the same for Muster's
Move orders and a bot army's Retreat to its base. So this change brings forward the approved unit AI rule "an ended
order holds" (`arrive` hold, its call 7) for these two orders: a group on Retreat or Move whose centre is within
`arriveRadius` (4 blocks, draft) of its destination fights as on Hold (from its slot, Hold's `engageRadius`) until its
next order. On the way it still fights nothing. The unit AI plan later replaces this with its full `arrive` column.

**A retreat under contact costs.** A retreating group has its back to its pursuers, so their blows land as flank or
rear hits (C16, C16s). That is the price of pulling out while engaged, and Retreat gets no exemption **(designer's
call)**; the unit AI's Fall back is the fighting withdrawal.

**Regroup lands with supply houses, not with this change** **(Kort)**. This design needs nothing from it: Retreat
already pulls a group out, so routing can go before Regroup exists. When Regroup lands it covers the base and every
held house at once, and when the player gives it, it plays the retreat sound to him (5.2).

### 7.2 Gilded Order

**Bulwark:** section 4.2. Card text: "Shield troops take 15% less ranged damage; your troops within 5 of a Gilded
troop take no extra flank damage." (8 and 12 at tiers II and III.)

**Oath Banner (Aldric) (Kort).** Unchanged: the trigger (his group's order turns to Hold, cooldown 600), the banner
block, 300 ticks, radius 12, +10% damage (`auraFactor`), the particles at the plant. The rally horn at the plant goes
(5.2). The banner block is placed by Aldric's ability; nobody uses it as an item. New: while it
stands, own troops inside its radius are **anchored**:
- no knockback from any troop source (slams, `knockback` statuses such as the Arbalist's *Heavy bolt*);
- no pull (the Harpooner's harpoon, Admiral Vael's undertow);
- no Slowness from any troop source (`onhit_slow`/`onhit_status` slow, Brine, Riptide, frostshot, blight, mist,
  ensnare); a slow already on a troop is removed when it enters the radius or the banner is planted
  **(designer's call)**.
- Stuns, `weaken` and `bleed` still land **(designer's call: "immovable" is about being moved)**.

This is what "hold the line" means once nobody breaks: the banner's ground cannot be taken from under it. It answers
Ironclad slams, Tidecaller harpoons and every slow. Level 2 (*Oath Banner*: 400 ticks, +15%) and level 3 (*Marshal's
sweep*) stay as the progression design has them. Card text: "On Hold, plants a banner for 15 s: your troops within 12
deal 10% more damage and cannot be knocked back, pulled or slowed."

Sibling check: no Gilded troop moves or roots enemies or friends (Squire shield and *Shield bash* stun, Archer steady,
Templar shield, Longbow pierce, Sunweaver mend). The immunity is new to the game.

**Templar *Unbroken* (level 3) (designer's call).** "At or below 30% of his max health he takes 20% less damage." A
wall that is hardest to finish. It shares the "wounded" test with `fearless` (7.4) but is defensive and Gilded; no
Gilded sibling has a damage-taken ability.

**Rally Horn becomes *Muster*** (Kort wants no horns; the name goes with them) **(designer's call on the name; the

### 7.4 Graveborn

- A Graveborn troop that rises through *Rise Again* stands at 30% health, so it rises already fearless: the trait and

- Grave Bloom's temporary Risen carry it, as they carry the Risen's ability today.
- Level 2 (progression design section 6): ***Fearless***: +20% → +30%, and 30% → 40% of max health. This replaces

- Card text: "Below 30% health he deals 20% more damage."

Sibling check: the Grave Reaper cleaves, the Barrow Stalker slows, the Gravecaller blights, the Hollow King dreads;

## 9. Bots, modes and the campaign

### 9.1 Bots
- **Phase 1 bots (built):** `Campaign.botDecide` already retreats a whole bot army by order when its living troops fall
  to `retreatShare` and returns at `rallyShare`. Unchanged, except that its charge no longer sounds a horn (5.2). With
  routing gone, that order is the only way a bot army leaves a fight.
- **Unit AI (approved, not planned):** the General keeps its doctrines' `fallBackShare` and `rallyShare` (orders). It
  watches "an enemy group seen retreating, a group of its own at its last stand" instead of "a rout or rally"; the council plans again on "a
  group wiped out" instead of "a rout". The Graveborn doctrine "presses the same objective after every regroup". Its
  Flank order strikes the aimed group in the flank arc (C16's), not "morale's rear arc".
- **Phase 4 mode bots:** each tier's retreat, rally (return), charge and shieldwall logic stays; they are orders.
