# In-match progression: levels from kills, gear bought in battle

Status: **approved by Kort as written, 2026-10-21** (after the builder check). Kort's answers from the brainstorm are marked **(Kort)**. Calls the
designer made where Kort did not rule are marked **(designer's call)**; Kort may overturn any of them. Nothing here is
built.

This design replaces the approved between-match evolutions (Houses spec `2026-10-17-houses-design.md` sections 2.6,
the evolve lines in 2.1 to 2.5, the evolve rows of 4.1 and 4.5, the stage-3 gate of 4.7, the stage share of the 4.4
cap, and the evolution goals in the 4.9 tables). Everything else in the Houses spec stands. It does not revive the
deleted growth design: nothing in this document persists between matches except what the Houses spec already keeps
(stars, shards, Mastery, Renown).

Sources: Kort's direction of 2026-10-21 (end of `docs/QUESTIONS-FOR-KORT.md`), the Houses spec, `sheets/design.json`
on `phase-2-houses` (f0febad: `economy`, `rarities`, `evolutions`, `recruitment`, `mastery`, `renown`, `abilities`,
`counters`, `botCommand`, `botBanners`), the evolution code on `phase-2-houses` and `phase-2-models`, the art pipeline
v2 design (`2026-10-21-art-pipeline-v2-design.md`), `docs/PHASE-2-BALANCE.md` and `docs/VISION-AND-ROADMAP.md`.

## 1. Intent

Kort wants troops to grow through fighting, not through gold spent in camp. A soldier who survives and kills gets
better in that match, and the player turns battle gold into better weapons and armour for the troops who earned it.
The between-match evolutions (stages bought with gold and shards, Mastery I gating stage 3) go away.

Why this is better for the game:
- Power is earned in the match you are playing, so a new player's banner is not weaker than a veteran player's
  banner by more than the small star ceiling. Tactics beat grind (vision, section 2).
- Veterans are visible: better weapons, better armour, rank chevrons. You see which enemy line is dangerous and
  which of your soldiers to protect (pillar 1, readable drama; pillar 2, your banner shows on the field).
- Battle gold, which winners bank today without anything to spend it on (about 1,000 per team at the end of a sim
  round), gets a sink that is a real choice.

### Goals
1. Every soldier has a level for the match: 1 when trained or rebought, 2 and 3 from kills. **(Kort)**
2. Each level up gives a stat bonus, the right to buy the next gear tier, and an ability: level 2 improves the
   troop's own ability, level 3 adds a second one. **(Kort)**
3. Three weapon tiers and three armour tiers per troop, bought separately with battle gold, each with its own look.
   **(Kort)**
4. A fully geared level-3 soldier is about 1.7 times a fresh one, before abilities. **(Kort)**
5. A side that is losing can still come back: killing veterans levels you faster. **(Kort)**

### Non-goals
- Nothing about levels or gear carries to the next match. No named soldiers, no soldier memory, no "Oath XP", no
  growth design.
- No gear for the player commander. The commander's kit and class are unchanged.
- No level 4 or higher, and no gear tier IV.
- No new currency. Gear costs battle gold (the squad's round gold).
- Supply houses. Kort wants capturable supply houses on every map (deploy, regroup, buy gear, heal). They get their
  own design right after this one **(Kort)**. This design only says where gear is bought until they exist (section
  5.3).
- No per-soldier shop screen. Gear is bought per troop type.

### Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)
- The first big fight decides the match because its winner snowballs on levels and gear.
- The player has to stand in a menu in the middle of a melee to keep his troops competitive.
- Veterans that cannot be told apart on the field.
- A level-3 ability that copies the signature ability of another troop of the same House (unit identity rule: a
  stat-identical pair is what the identity pass removed).

## 2. Constraints

- Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric. Every number lives in `sheets/design.json`; `Spec.java` is generated by
  `tools/preflight.py` and never hand-edited; preflight must print `Preflight clean`.
- **Art:** each troop's weapon tier and armour tier are separate art: three weapon models and three armour looks
  per troop, made by the art pipeline v2 rules (Blockbench sources, the style guide, LabPBR maps, the 128-quad weapon
  cap, the texture budgets). Kort's art-references ruling of 2026-10-21 applies: weapon and armour look visibly
  stronger at each tier.
- The counter table (`Combat.resolve`) stays the one place damage multipliers meet. Level, gear and status effects
  enter it as rows or multipliers, not as side paths.
- Harness and checks stay in `test-tools/`. Client and commit rules of `docs/HANDOFF.md` apply.
- The progression ceiling between matches stays +12% over the sheet base (vision, section 2). Levels and gear are
  battle effects, outside that cap, like House traits and commander effects (Houses spec 4.4).

## 3. Levels

### 3.1 Thresholds and stat bonus

| Level | Kills needed (from level 1) | Health and damage bonus | Gear tier it unlocks | Ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 (trained or rebought) | none | tier I (worn from training) | the troop's own ability |
| 2 | 3 | +5% | tier II | own ability improved (section 6) |
| 3 | 8 | +10% | tier III | a second ability added (section 6) |

**(Kort)** for the thresholds and the bonus. Kills carry over: a soldier who gets 9 kill credits at once (killing a
level-3 enemy with two helpers is still 3 each, see 3.2) can go from level 1 to level 3 in one blow, and gets both
level ups.

The thresholds are starting values. The bot sims tune them (section 9) toward this target **(Kort)**:
- the first level-2 soldier of a side appears 2 to 3 minutes into a 15-minute round;
- at the end of a round, about a third of the winning army's living soldiers are level 2 or higher;
- a few level 3s appear per round, not none and not a dozen.

When a soldier levels up, his max health rises by the bonus and his current health rises by the same amount (the level
up heals only what it adds) **(designer's call)**. A level is lost only with the soldier: a disbanded or killed soldier
is gone, and his replacement starts at level 1 **(Kort)**. A Graveborn soldier who rises again through *Rise Again*
is the same soldier: he keeps his level, kill credits and gear **(designer's call)**.

### 3.2 Who gets a kill **(Kort)**

When an enemy troop dies, each of these soldiers of the killer's team gets kill credit, whichever allied army he
belongs to; a soldier who took no part gets nothing **(designer's call)**:
- the soldier who landed the killing blow;
- every own soldier who damaged, slowed, pulled or weakened the victim, or held it in one of his zones (mist, blight
  cloud), in the last 10 seconds (`progression.assistSeconds`);
- every own soldier who healed or buffed (mend, howl, oath banner, an aura) the killer or one of those helpers in the
  last 10 seconds.

Each of them gets as many kills as the victim's level: a level-1 victim counts 1, level 2 counts 2, level 3 counts 3
(the veteran bounty). This is the catch-up rule: a losing side that brings down veterans levels its own soldiers fast.

Further rules **(designer's calls)**:
- An enemy commander (a player or a bot's commander body) counts as a level-3 victim (`progression.commanderBounty`
  3).
- The player commander never levels and never takes credit. If the commander lands a killing blow, his helpers still
  get theirs.
- Temporary troops (Grave Bloom Risen and the Gravecaller's level-3 Risen) never earn kills or levels. Killing one
  counts as a level-1 victim.
- Structures (the Forward Supply banner, Phase 4 gates and walls) are not kills.
- Mastery (Houses spec 4.7) still counts killing blows per troop type, exactly as it does today. Assists and the
  bounty are for levels only.

The code today keeps one `lastHitBy` squad per unit. Assists need a short per-victim log of (attacking unit, time) and
a per-unit log of (supporting unit, time) for heals and buffs, both trimmed to the last 10 seconds. The shape is the
builder's.

### 3.3 Level names

Commons and Rares take their old evolve-line names as level names: the Order Squire is "Order Squire" at level 1,
"Knight" at level 2 and "Knight-Captain" at level 3 (Houses spec 2.1 to 2.5 has all 20 lines). Epics and Legendaries
keep their own name at every level **(designer's call; Kort chose the feed line "Knight-Captain rises" with this
naming)**. The names live in the `evolutions` sheet, cut down to `id, troop, level, name` and renamed `levelNames`
(section 8).

## 4. Gear

### 4.1 Tiers and what they do **(Kort)**

| Tier | Weapon | Armour | Who wears it |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | the troop's starting weapon | the troop's starting armour | every soldier, from training |
| II | +10% to the troop's main output | +10% max health | soldiers at level 2 or higher, once bought |
| III | +20% to the troop's main output | +20% max health | soldiers at level 3, once bought |

"Main output" is the troop's damage; for the Sunweaver (no damage) it is the heal amount; for the Mist Caller (no
damage) it is the cloud's duration **(designer's call)**. Blight, shell and slam damage scale with it like a hit.
Armour raises max health and current health by the same amount, like a level up **(designer's call: health, not a
damage reduction, so the number shows on cards and health bars and does not stack strangely with Bulwark and
Entrench)**.

All percentages add with the level bonus into one multiplier per stat: a level-3 soldier with Weapon III and Armour III
deals +30% damage and has +30% health (1.69 times a fresh soldier in health times damage). Stars, House traits,
commander effects and counter rows multiply on top, as they do today.

### 4.2 Buying **(Kort)**

- Gear is bought **once per troop type, for the round**, per army. The four buys are Weapon II, Weapon III,
  Armour II and Armour III for each troop type in the banner.
- A tier II buy opens when the first soldier of that type reaches level 2 this round; a tier III buy opens when the
  first one reaches level 3 and tier II of that track is bought. An opened buy stays open for the round, even if
  that soldier dies.
- Once bought, a tier is worn by every soldier of that type whose level allows it: at once by those already there,
  and later by each soldier the moment he levels up. A soldier wears `min(his level, the highest tier bought)` on each
  track. A rebought soldier wears tier I until he levels again **(Kort)**. A soldier who levels before the player
  buys keeps his old gear until the buy **(Kort)**.
- No refunds, and no gear cost on rebuys: the bought tier stays bought for the round.
- Allied players in the same team do not share buys: each army has its own armoury **(designer's call)**.
- Commander class discounts (the Warlock's cheaper magic troops) do not apply to gear **(designer's call)**.

### 4.3 Prices **(Kort)**

The price depends on the troop's rarity and the tier, and is the same for weapon and armour:

| Rarity | Tier II | Tier III | Both tracks, both tiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 40 | 80 | 240 |
| Rare | 60 | 120 | 360 |
| Epic | 80 | 160 | 480 |
| Legendary | 100 | 200 | 600 |

For scale: in the bot sims one army earns about 1,750 gold in a 15-minute round (passive 5 gold per 5 s, plus held
points). Maxing a typical five-type banner (`gilded_normal`: two Commons, two Rares, one Epic) costs 1,680, so a winning
army upgrades most of its banner over a round and a losing army chooses one or two types. The prices go into the
`rarities` sheet (section 8) and are tuned by the sims.

### 4.4 Looks

Each tier is its own art (section 2): three weapon models and three armour looks per troop.
- **Armour.** The stage skins and stage parts the art pipeline already makes (`<troop>_s1.png` to `_s3.png`, the parts'
  `stages` key, the relief layers) become armour tiers I to III. Commons and Rares already have three; the ten Epics
  and Legendaries have one and need tiers II and III.
- **Weapons.** Today a troop has one weapon (`art/weapons/<id>`). It becomes tier I; tiers II and III are new models
  under the same rules. Troops with no held weapon (the Moot Hound, Web Stalker, Old Horn, Brassjaw, the Brute, the
  Mist Caller, the Mortar) show the weapon tier as a set of parts on the rig, keyed by weapon tier the way stage parts
  are keyed today: tusk caps, claw guards, a jaw plate, a heavier barrel, a conch or staff **(designer's call; the
  shapes are the art step's)**.
- The Mastery II veteran look (Houses spec 4.7) becomes a trim overlay that sits on top of whichever armour tier is
  worn, not a fourth full skin **(designer's call: otherwise it needs three veteran skins per troop)**.

**0.5.0 waits for all tier art (Kort, builder-check answer):** every troop ships with its own weapon I to III and
armour I to III. The art is made in Phase 2's art steps (11.3), each item through Kort's art-reference review. During
development only, a tier whose art does not exist yet shows as the tier below it, and the chevrons still show the
level (section 7); stats never wait for art. A check fails the release build while any troop is still on that
stopgap.

## 5. Where gear is bought

### 5.1 Armoury tab

The in-battle barracks screen gets an **Armoury** tab: one row per troop type in the banner, with the troop's
portrait at its current best worn tier and four buttons (Weapon II, Weapon III, Armour II, Armour III). Each button is
in one of four states: locked ("needs a level-2 Knight", with the level name), open (price shown), bought, or
unaffordable (open but greyed, price shown). The look follows the approved War Table and Iron Ledger family; the
`ui-ux-pro-max` skill is used only for UX checks (contrast, hit targets, feedback).

A buy is a server command, `gear:<troop>:<weapon|armour>:<tier>`, checked on the server against the opening rule,
the price and the location rule, like `manage` checks training today.

The barracks screen opens today only by right-clicking the barracks lectern. The Armoury tab also opens anywhere
with a key (the builder picks a free one), so the player can see what is open and what it costs from the field; its
buy buttons work only where the location rule allows, and elsewhere are greyed with the reason ("Buy at your barracks
or a held point") **(designer's call)**. No new object is placed on the maps for this.

### 5.2 The final rule **(Kort)**

Gear is bought at the army's barracks or at a supply house the army's team holds. The supply house design defines
the houses.

### 5.3 Until supply houses exist **(designer's call)**

Gear is bought where the army can reinforce: within `economy.accessRadius` of its barracks, or near one of the team's
current reinforcement anchors (a held point that passes the forward-deploy rule, or a standing Forward Supply banner
with no enemy near it). That is `Battle.reinforcementAnchors(team)` today. The supply house design replaces or extends
this list. The rule keeps the shop out of open melee and makes holding points worth a little more.

Bots ignore the location rule, as they already skip walking to the barracks to train **(designer's call)**.

## 6. Abilities per level **(Kort: level 2 improves the troop's own ability, level 3 adds a second one)**

Rules for the table:
- **Level 2** replaces the troop's ability row with a stronger copy of the same mechanic. Values below are the step
  from today's sheet row (f0febad) to the level-2 row; if the base row is retuned, the step is kept.
- **Level 3** keeps the level-2 row and adds a second ability row.
- No level-3 ability copies the signature ability of a sibling in the same House. A few reuse another House's
  mechanic; that is allowed, and noted.
- Bots use every passive and triggered ability automatically, as they do today.

### 6.1 Small new mechanics the table needs

Each is small and is used by more than one troop where possible. Everything else in the table is an existing
mechanic (`shield`, `stance`, `pierce`, `cleave`, `onhit_slow`, `pounce`, `aura`, `slam`, `fearless`, `mist`, `mend`,
`blight`).

| Id | What it is | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| N1 `onhit_status` | `onhit_slow` generalised: a hit applies a status (`slow`, `weaken`, `bleed`) or a knockback, with the existing cooldown, ticks and level columns. `onhit_slow` rows become `onhit_status` with status `slow`. | many |
| N2 `weaken` status | The target deals 15% less damage for its duration. Read in `Combat.resolve` as a multiplier on the target's outgoing hits. Not the vanilla Weakness effect (troop damage does not read it). | Bone Archer, Shieldbreaker, Mist Caller |
| N3 `bleed` status | 1 damage every 20 ticks for its duration, dealt through the troop damage type so undead are not immune (vanilla Poison would be). | Risen, Web Stalker, Harpooner, Mortar |
| N4 `stun` on a single-target slam | A slam with radius 0: knocks back and stuns only the target (stuns exist today for slams). | Order Squire |
| N5 `onkill_heal` | The soldier heals a share of his max health when he lands a killing blow. | Grave Reaper |
| N6 `raise` | An enemy killed by this soldier's blight rises as a temporary Risen of his army for 15 s, at most 2 at a time. Reuses Grave Bloom's temporary troop code. | Gravecaller |
| N7 `rise` | This soldier may rise once at a share of max health (the Rise Again path through `ALLOW_DEATH`), with its own chance, adding to the House trait's chance where both apply. A Legendary may take it although the trait excludes Legendaries. | Risen, Bone Archer, Hollow King |
| N8 `mark` | Hits mark the target for a duration; a marked target takes extra damage from the army's troops. Reuses the Warden's Mark Prey state, without the glow. | Hide Hunter |
| N9 stance condition `target_routing_or_slowed` | A new condition in the counter table's closed list. | Moot Hound |
| N10 tag `thickhide` | A new counter row C19: any shot (`arrow`, `bolt`) against `thickhide` x0.75. | Old Horn, Kraken-touched Brute |
| N11 aura `rally` and `damageTaken` | An aura may also rally routing own groups in its radius (`Morale.rally`, as the Oath Banner does), or lower the damage own troops in its radius take. | Bloodhowl Seer, Brassjaw |
| N12 zone `status` | A `mist` zone may carry a status instead of Slowness and may skip the ranged penalty. | Ironhold Mortar, Mist Caller |
| N13 mend `cleanse` | A mend also removes `slow`, `weaken` and `bleed` from its target. | Sunweaver |

Statuses of the same kind on one target do not stack: the longer remaining duration wins (as Brine and Riptide do
today).

## 7. On screen **(Kort)**

- **Level up, on the soldier:** a short flash of particles in the side's colour and a sound, both readable within
  about 24 blocks. His hidden custom name changes to the level name.
- **Rank chevrons:** one chevron over a level-2 soldier, two over a level-3, for **both teams**, so the player sees the
  enemy's veterans. Drawn by the troop renderer as a small billboard above the head, in the side's colour, within
  `progression.chevronRange` (24 blocks, the troop-depth near distance). Not affected by the troop-depth Visuals
  switch (it is information, not depth) **(designer's call)**.
- **Iron Ledger group cards:** per group, pips for how many living soldiers are at level 2 and at level 3, and a glint
  on the card when an Armoury buy for a troop in that group is open and not yet bought.
- **Event feed:** one line when any soldier of either side reaches level 3, with the level name ("Knight-Captain
  rises"; an enemy's line in the enemy colour). Level 2 makes no feed line. Uses the UI plan's feed with a new kind
  and glyph.
- **Camp troop cards:** the "Stage n of 3" line goes; the card shows the three level names and the three ability lines.
- **End-of-match report:** per troop type, the highest level reached and how many soldiers reached level 3; the gear
  tiers bought. The report JSON gains a `levels` block.

The client needs, per soldier: level, worn weapon tier and worn armour tier. Today the stage travels as the custom
name, which cannot carry three numbers cleanly; the builder picks the sync (tracked data on the troop mobs or a
packet). Per army it needs the Armoury state (opened and bought buys) for the tab and the glint, in the state packet.

## 8. Data

Column names and sheet shapes are the builder's, within these rules. Values are the starting values above.

- **`progression`** (new, one row): `enabled` (bool, default true), `assistSeconds` 10, `commanderBounty` 3,
  `chevronRange` 24.0, `feedLevel` 3.
- **`levels`** (new, three rows): `level`, `kills` (0, 3, 8), `healthPct` and `damagePct` (0, 0.05, 0.10).
- **`gearTiers`** (new, three rows): `tier`, `weaponPct` (0, 0.10, 0.20), `armourPct` (0, 0.10, 0.20).
- **`rarities`**: new columns `gearPrice2` and `gearPrice3` (40/80, 60/120, 80/160, 100/200). Common and Rare
  `starPct2`/`starPct3` become 0.06/0.12 (section 10).
- **`evolutions`** becomes **`levelNames`**: `id, troop, level, name`, the same 40 rows with the gold, shard, percent
  and mastery columns gone. Internal ids that name it (`troops.evolveLine`) may keep their names, as `writ` did;
  nothing a player reads says "stage" or "evolve".
- **`levelAbilities`** (new, 60 rows): `troop, level (2 or 3), ability` (a reference into `abilities`). The level-2
  row replaces the troop's base ability; the level-3 row is added.
- **`abilities`**: 60 new rows (section 6), and the columns N1 to N13 need (a status, its flags). `onhit_slow` rows
  become `onhit_status`.
- **`counters`**: C19 (`thickhide`), the condition `target_routing_or_slowed`, and the bodkin row.
- **`botCommand`**: `gearMaxTier`, `gearReserveTroops`, `gearPriority` (section 9).
- **`mastery`**: `mastery_1.reward` loses `evolve:3`.
- **`recruitment.capPct`** stays 0.12 and now covers stars alone.

Preflight rules: every Common and Rare has level names for 2 and 3; every troop has exactly one level-2 and one
level-3 row; a level-2 ability has the same mechanic as the base it replaces; level kills rise; gear prices are
positive and tier III above tier II; `starPct3 <= capPct` (the old "star plus stage" check becomes stars only).

## 9. Bots, balance and the gate

### 9.1 How bots buy **(Kort)**

In the bot economy tick (every 100 ticks, `Campaign.tick`), after refilling the banner as today, a bot buys gear by
its difficulty row:

| Difficulty | Highest tier it buys | Gold it keeps for rebuys | Order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | II | the cost of 8 of its banner's cheapest troop | banner order |
| Normal | III | 5 troops | banner order; ranged and siege types weapon first, infantry and cavalry armour first, support weapon first |
| Hard | III | 2 troops | each buy as soon as it opens, starting with the troop type that has the most soldiers at level 2 or higher |

Bots follow the same opening rule as players and ignore the location rule (5.3). This gives Hard a real edge that does
not depend on tactics knobs, which never separated the tiers (questions file, problem 5).

### 9.2 Balance gate **(Kort)**

- Phase 2's House gates carry on exactly as today (20 matches, 300 s rounds, 40 to 60%). Levels and gear do not exist
  on that branch yet, so nothing changes there.
- This design's plan ends with a re-gate of all five pure normal banners, each against the current best pure banner,
  at full 900 s rounds (300 s rounds barely reach level 3), 20 matches each, 10 per seat, run twice: once with
  `progression.enabled` false (the baseline) and once true with bot gear buying on.
- **What it gates (designer's call, from the builder check):** absolute House balance (40 to 60%) belongs to Phase 2's
  balance pass, which already has open failures (`graveborn_normal` against `gilded_normal` in
  `docs/PHASE-2-BALANCE.md`); levels and gear cannot fix a House that misses the band without them. This gate
  measures what levels change. Pass: for every House, its share with levels on is within 10 points of its own
  baseline share. A House that also sits inside 40 to 60% in both runs is reported as balanced; one outside it goes
  back to the Phase 2 balance pass, not to this plan.
- **Snowball check:** each sim logs the score at 300 s. The comeback rate is the share of decided matches won by the
  side that trailed at 300 s, pooled over all 100 matches of a condition (20 per House is too few on its own). Pass:
  the pooled rate with levels is no more than 10 points below the pooled baseline **(designer's call on the margin)**.
- **Cost:** about 3 hours of machine time per House per condition, about 30 hours in all, run in the background two
  servers at a time; headless servers do not count toward the client limit.
- **Stop rule:** as in Phase 2, two tuning rounds; whatever still fails after them goes to Kort in the questions file
  with the numbers, and the plan reports instead of looping.
- **Level check:** the sims log, per side, when its first level-2 and level-3 soldier appeared, and the share of
  living soldiers at level 2 or higher at the end. The thresholds (3.1) are tuned toward the target there.
- Tuning order when a check fails: thresholds, then gear prices, then gear percentages, then level bonuses. A
  catch-up gold rule for the losing side is not in this design; if the levers above cannot pass the snowball check,
  it goes to Kort as a question.
- Difficulty ladder (Easy < Normal < Hard): report only, since Kort parked the tier ladder until the Phase 3 maps.

## 13. Decisions in this document

**Kort's answers (brainstorm, 2026-10-21):**
1. Gear is bought once per troop type for the round.
2. Kill credit: killer, helpers in the last 10 s and healers of the killer; a victim counts its level.
3. Gear is bought at the barracks or at a held supply house; supply houses get their own design next. "Level up
   troops" at a supply house means buying gear there; levels still come instantly from kills.
4. Level 2 improves the troop's own ability; level 3 adds a second one.
5. Thresholds 3 and 8 kills, tuned by sims toward the level target.
6. Gear prices by rarity: 40/80, 60/120, 80/160, 100/200.
7. Power: level +5/+10%, weapon +10/+20% damage, armour +10/+20% health.
8. Shards, stars, Mastery and Renown stay; evolutions go; Common and Rare stars rise to +6/+12%; Mastery I is a title
   only.
9. On screen: level-up flash, chevrons on both teams, Iron Ledger pips and glint, a feed line at level 3.
10. Bots buy by difficulty (Easy II only with a big reserve, Normal II and III, Hard as soon as open).
11. Re-gate all five Houses at full length with a snowball check at the end of this plan.
12. Lands in 0.5.0, after Phase 2 Task 10; Phase 2 drops the evolve parts of Tasks 11 and 12 now.
13. (Builder check) 0.5.0 waits until every troop has its own weapon and armour art for tiers I to III.

**Designer's calls (Kort may overturn):** the level-up heal rule (3.1); a risen Graveborn keeps level and gear; the
