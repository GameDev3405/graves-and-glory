# UI and HUD overhaul: design

**Date:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** for Kort's review.
**Implements:** Kort's UI decisions of 2026-10-17 (round 2) and 2026-10-18 (round 3) in `docs/QUESTIONS-FOR-KORT.md`.
**Sources:** `work/ui-concepts/` v4 and v4.1 (`v4-hud-iron-ledger`, `v4-ui-war-table-home`, `v4-ui-war-table-recruit`, `CONCEPTS.md`, `DESIGN-RULES.md`, `GRADING-RUBRIC.md`, the `v4a_*.py` painters) and `work/ui-concepts/title/` ("The Hill", `TITLE.md`).
**Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-ui-plan.md`.
**Retires:** Phase 1 Task 8 (HUD cleanup, glyphs, key-bound hints). Its goals live here.

## 1. Intent

The game's screens are still charcoal boxes of text. Over three rounds Kort approved one object per place: in battle, an iron-and-vellum ledger at the edges of the view (Iron Ledger); in camp, an oak war table with brass and parchment (War Table); at launch, a commander with the Dawn banner on a hill over a real battlefield (The Hill). This spec turns those mockups into the game at the same pixels, with live data, and adds no mechanics.

It matters now because pillar 1 (readable drama) is judged on the HUD, pillar 2 (your banner is yours) on the recruit and banner screens, and pillar 5 (honest numbers) says no screen is done until a screenshot from a running game shows it.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals**

1. At 1920x1080 each in-scope screen matches its mockup to the pixel (positions, sizes, type, colours, copy), with live data in place of mock data.
2. One material per place, from one exported kit of textures; no vanilla grey widgets on our screens.
3. A battle reads at a glance: score and time, who holds each point, each of my groups, my ability, what just happened.
4. Meaning never rests on colour alone; every key hint names the player's actual binding.
5. No hunger: no food row, and food never limits a player.
6. Other window sizes work: nothing overlaps, nothing is off screen, nothing throws.

**Non-goals**

- New mechanics. Houses, rarity, stars, pity, shards and commander actives are Phase 2; this work draws them where the data exists and hides them where it does not.
- The end-of-match report (Phase 1 Task 7 keeps its table), the M map (glyphs only), the campaign briefing card, the Melty and LAN flows behind "Play with friends", public battles, other languages.

**Anti-goals** (what this must not become)

- Pixel art drawn at a fractional scale.
- A busy HUD: no minimap, gold, queue or key strip in battle (the v2 audit removed them), no fact twice.
- A spinning panorama, or a painted fake of the battlefield: L0 is a real capture and only sways.
- Colour-only states, or hints that hardcode default keys.
- Placeholder data presented as real.

## 3. Constraints

**Canvas.** Layouts are authored on a 960x540 canvas at GUI scale 2 (1920x1080). Our screens and HUD draw at `k` screen pixels per design pixel, `k = min(guiScale, max(1, floor(min(fbW/960, fbH/540))))`: 2 at 1080p and 1440p, 4 at 4K, 1 at 1366x768. A lower GUI Scale shrinks our UI; a larger one is ignored, so the art stays sharp.

- The canvas is the framebuffer divided by k (960x540 at 1080p, 1280x720 at 1440p). Elements anchor to its edges as the mockups place them.
- War Table pages centre a 960x540 layout under a full-width rail, on a table painted at 1280x720.
- Vanilla hearts, XP bar and hotbar keep the player's GUI Scale; the ability slot anchors to the hotbar and shares its scale.
- Mouse coordinates and scissor rectangles convert through k (DrawContext's scissor ignores the matrix stack).

**Art.**

- Every texture is painted at scale 1 by the mockups' own painters (light from the top left, the ramps in `v4pal.py`), copied once into `tools/ui_art/` because `work/` is not in git. `tools/build_ui_art.py` exports them deterministically into `src/main/resources/assets/gravesandglory/textures/gui/` with an `atlas.json`: size, draw offset (shadows sit outside the box) and kind (fixed, 9-slice, 3-slice or sheet).
- Textures are painted at the exact size a layout uses. Slicing is kept for parts whose length varies at runtime (spine, rail, banner rod, key chips, vellum buttons, plaques); edges and middles tile, never stretch. Bars fill by drawing part of a full bar.

**Text.** All text goes through Minecraft's TextRenderer with the mod's font providers, as DarkUi does today: 16 px Marcellus (new `font/focal.json`) for the one focal line per screen, 11 px Marcellus (`title`) for headings and names, 11 px Lato (`body`) for body copy, and the default Minecraft font at 1x (8 px) for numbers, labels, chips and keys. Colours are the mockup tokens. On dark: ivory #eee9df, muted #afb9bc, brass #c8a56b. On vellum: ink #2a2219, sepia #5c4a32. Text never sits on busy pixels, and long text truncates with "...".

**Portraits.**

Live models drawn with `InventoryScreen.drawEntity` (quaternion overload) in a scissored window, framed by unit identity rule 8 as amended by v4: chest-up, 20 model pixels from 4 above the head to 8 below the shoulders, yaw 30°, camera 5° below eye level, fixed gaze; "Tall +N" adds N rows. Windows are 40 px in rolls and party seats (2 px per model pixel) and 160 px in the recruit letter (8 px per model pixel). No team colour.

**Title background.** The vanilla panorama mechanism (`CubeMapRenderer`) draws six faces we capture in game with Complementary Reimagined at golden hour. The view sways ±1.5° of yaw over 48 s and never spins.

**Code rules** carry over from Phase 1: game numbers live in sheets (presentation timings may be named constants); packet changes bump the channel id (`state_v6` to `state_v7`, new `feed_v1`); harness code lives only in `test-tools/`.

## 4. Screens in scope

### 4.1 Title, "The Hill"

Six layers, back to front, per TITLE.md: L0 the capture; L1 a 14-arrow volley about every 12 s; L2 the hill (960x300 with 12 px overscan, 1.5x the L0 offset); L3 the commander with the Dawn banner (180x300, four wind frames of 3 ticks, moving with L2); L4 embers and ash (34 and 30 alive by day, 64 and 18 by night, rising up-right at 6 to 14 px/s, fading over 3 to 5 s, kept off the lockup, menu and version); L5 the UI: the brass lockup at (240, 24), the tagline "Collect an army. Command the battlefield." and a text column at x 40: "Ride to camp" (16 px, focused by default), "Play with friends", "Settings", "Credits", "Quit".

The hovered line turns brass-hi #dfbd77 and carries the spearhead marker. The horn sits beside "Play with friends" and sounds `horn_rally` once per entry. The version sits bottom right in muted grey.

Scene layers cover the screen (scaled up, cropped from the top and sides); the UI sits on the canvas. Sounds follow TITLE.md, with a new `title_ambience` loop. Night is the same drawing in the night palette over a night capture.

### 4.2 War Table, home

- The oak table under a brass-inlaid rail, with plaques: Camp (Esc, closes the table), Recruit, Banner, Contracts (wax count), Chapter, Friends (wax count).
- Party seats on bare oak at the left: live models, names, ready checks, an Invite seat.
- Three order sheets, Practice, Skirmish and Campaign, each with its ink drawing, name and subtitle ("train against bots", "one battle, your rules", "the story, battle by battle"). The chosen one is raised, ribboned and sealed. Focus follows the pointer and the arrow keys (Kort, 2026-10-18): the sheet under the pointer, or the keyboard focus, is the raised sealed one, and the other two settle to their flat size with no seal. The handover animates so the table feels alive: the leaving sheet eases flat over 150 ms, the arriving sheet rises over 150 ms (ease-out) and its seal stamps down in the last 100 ms with a one-frame squash and `ui.book.page_turn` at volume 0.3; the ribbon settles two frames behind the seal. Idle life, all cheap sprite loops: the candle flame flickers on a 4-frame, 6-tick loop and its glow breathes; the quill tip twitches every 4 to 7 s; the brass Ride plate glints once when it gains focus; the party seats keep their live-model idle. Nothing moves under the pointer except the sheet it is over.
- The field card: map name, map, rules ("Domination to 300", "2 v 2, Normal bots"), a Change button.
- One brass Ride plate: the chosen mode's primary action and its key (Enter).

### 4.3 War Table, recruit

- **Banners** hang from a brass rod; the chosen one hangs longer. In Phase 1 the factions stand in for Houses: Dawn on the Gilded cloth, Dusk on the Graveborn cloth, labelled with the faction names. The other three appear once Phase 2 data exists.
- **Muster roll** (left): one 56 px row per troop of the House: live 40 px portrait, name (ink if owned, sepia if not), stars (Phase 2).
- **Letter** (centre): the selected or newly revealed troop as a 160 px live portrait in the ornate frame, its name at 16 px and one line about it. A troop not yet recruited can be chosen here for the current price.
- **Ladder** (right, one axis): the sealed letters still to open, Enlist (brass plate, Enter, cost), the purse, the pity line ("Epic within 28 letters" in Phase 2; new troops left in Phase 1), Odds one click away.
- **Short of gold:** Enlist becomes an iron plate reading "Need 100" and does nothing.

### 4.4 Banner, Contracts, Chapter, Friends

The same table, rail and back plaque, from the same kit at lower detail and with no new ink drawings: Banner is the army deployment and commander class; Contracts are pinned vellum notes with progress bars and claim buttons; Chapter is the founders ladder; Friends is the party, the private room and invitations. Each keeps today's actions. The field picker behind Change uses the same kit.

### 4.5 Battle HUD, "Iron Ledger"

- **Ledger bar** (top centre): a vellum score card between the Dawn boss (round, sun) and the Dusk boss (diamond, crescent): Dawn score, track, clock, track, Dusk score.
- **Point seals** hang from the bar on chains, letter underneath, one sprite per state: Dawn (round, teal), Dusk (diamond, violet), contested (split square, crossed swords), neutral (bare iron square). Kingsfield's five fit on the same 28 px pitch. While an order is aimed at a point, its name shows under its seal. Capture progress stays in the world and on the M map, as mocked.
- **Group cards** hang from the iron spine at the bottom left, one per group that has fielded troops: name, state word (Fighting with its glyph, Steady, Fallen), strength bar, alive/full count, order and formation glyphs. States:
  - Loss flash: a red rule and the lost stretch of the bar lit, for 450 ms.
  - Selected: brass-bound on a brass hook, with glyph plus word for order and formation, and the key row "G Attack, V Follow, B Formation".
  - Routing: grey, the ROUTING stamp, a red count, faded glyphs.

  When the whole army is selected, every card is brass and the bottom card carries the key row.
- **Ability slot** beside the hotbar: picture, key on a brass plate, a dark clockwise sweep over the time still to run with a bright leading edge, seconds left. Hidden while the player has no ability (all of Phase 1).
- **Event feed** (right edge, v4.1): a vellum leaf of the ledger bound in iron, newest line at the bottom, five lines at most. A line lives 8 s and fades over its last 2 s; a sixth pushes the oldest out. Each line is a glyph and a short line in a dark ink of its hue:

| Event | Who sees it | Glyph | Ink | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| My team takes a point | everyone | seal, in my team's hue | team ink | "Dawn took Mill Hamlet" |
| The enemy takes a point | everyone | struck seal, in their hue | team ink | "Dusk took River Shrine" |
| My troops are lost (per troop, per 2 s) | owner | skull | red #7a2416 | "3 Dawn Knights lost" |
| Enemy troops my army killed | the killer's owner | grey skull | grey #504b44 | "4 Dusk Revenants lost" |
| A commander falls / returns | teammates | fallen / raised banner | brass #684600 | "Ansel's banner has fallen" / "Ansel is back in the field" |
| My group routs / regroups | owner | rout stamp | grey #504b44 | "Support routing" / "Ranged regrouped" |
| My troops deploy; server notices | owner | my emblem (sun or crescent) | ink #3d3124 | "Dawn Knights deploying" |

The team inks are Dawn #1d4f4c and Dusk #563a73. The feed replaces the notice slip and the action-bar deploy message.

- **Key hints.** Keys sit inside what they trigger: the selected card's key row, H on the ability slot. Other hints (Map, Commands, turning the facing, confirm and cancel while aiming) show only while the hints key (Left Alt by default) is held, in a round's first 60 s, or while an order is aimed: then every card shows its key row and a hint slip above the cards lists the rest. Key names come from the bindings; mouse buttons read LMB, RMB, MMB.
- **Unchanged:** vanilla hearts, XP bar, hotbar, crosshair. No minimap; M opens the full map.

## 5. Hunger removal

On a Graves and Glory server the food row is not drawn: a client mixin skips the three food-icon draws in `InGameHud.renderStatusBars` (air bubbles and mount health untouched). The server pins every player's food at 20, saturation at 0 and exhaustion at 0, in camp and in battle. With full food and no saturation, vanilla regeneration still heals slowly (1 HP about every 4 s) and food cannot be eaten. Worlds without the mod's server keep vanilla hunger.

## 6. Colour-blind rules

1. Every team colour travels with a shape: sun and round for Dawn, crescent and diamond for Dusk, split square with swords for contested, bare square for neutral. In the feed, seal versus struck seal says ours versus theirs.
2. Every state carries a word or shape as well as a colour: Fighting has its glyph, routing the stamp and the word ROUTING, a loss flash the lost stretch of the bar, selection the brass hook and key row.
3. Alarm red appears only with a shape or word: the skull, the stamp, "Need 100".
4. All text holds 4.5:1 against what sits behind it; the feed inks hold 5.2:1 or better on the slip.
5. A greyscale copy of the battle HUD screenshot still tells every point's owner and both scores apart.

## 7. Left to the builder

Within the mockups and rules: the painted pixels of textures the mockups never drew (the neutral seal; the glyphs move, charge, retreat, wedge and loose; the crescent feed glyph; the iron Enlist plate; the sheets of 4.4); the 4.4 layouts inside the plan's regions; volley paths and ember rates within the ranges; the title ambience mix; the panorama camera spot and pitch, as long as the composition matches `title-hill-day.png`; sound volumes TITLE.md does not name.
