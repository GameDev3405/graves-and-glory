Graves and Glory 0.5.0 preview 1 — throwaway preview build
October 21, 2026

A playable build of everything committed so far, for Kort to play and take
notes on. It is not the real 0.5.0 and nothing in it is published. Read
docs/PREVIEW-0.5.md for the install steps and the rough edges.

New: Gilded Order troops with their own models and stage art, six commander
classes (Champion, Reaver, Warden, Marshal, Warlock, Quartermaster) with the
H ability key, working Stand Fast and Rally Horn, five built maps, and the
War Table camp home and recruit page.

Known: four class abilities are not wired yet, the recruit page's Choose
button is refused, only Gilded has its own art, balance is untouched, and
windows under 960 by 540 crop the new pages.

Checked in the development client only: the battle self-test passes 11 of 11.
This build has not been installed into a launcher.
