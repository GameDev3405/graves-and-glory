EXISTING FOUNDATIONS
- Walkable lobby with recruitment hall, armoury, troop displays and war table.
- Twenty-unit deployment editing and permanent troop-type unlocks.
- Twelve troop types: six starters and six initially locked.
- Roll cost 100 collection gold; chooses uniformly among remaining locked
  troop types, with no duplicates. Fully unlocked collection stops troop rolls.
- Regular match completion 80 gold, plus 40 for victory; practice 40 gold.
  Current minimum qualifying duration is three minutes.
- Three commander classes with bonuses to matching troop roles.
- Four battlefields, three objectives each, tactical orders, formations,
  scouting/map pings, barracks training and bot commanders.
- Practice and 1v1 through 4v4 mode foundations; up to 160 troops.
- Existing shaders, texture choices, EMF/ETF, animations and equipment geometry.
- Skippable cinematic entrance and combat feedback.

NEWER SOURCE FEATURES — NEED RELEASE/BEHAVIOR VERIFICATION
- Two faction ready pads, up to two humans per side.
- Ten-second countdown after at least two admitted humans are ready.
- Quick match forces size=2 and bot fill; map selection is random.
- Stepping off cancels the countdown.
- One custom room at a time, with invitations expiring after sixty seconds.
- Custom leader controls map/mode/bot fill and start.

GAPS AND LIMITATIONS
- No daily/weekly contract system or battle pass found.
- No map voting; custom maps are leader-selected, quick maps random.
- No public matchmaking or central official progression service found.
- Current custom room is not a persistent public-queue party.
- Returning to the lobby closes the custom room.
- An open custom room disables ready pads globally on that server.
- Battle state and map selection are shared rather than isolated per room.
- Full-match admission can disconnect a new visitor with text suggesting
  waiting in a lobby, instead of actually admitting them to a waiting area.
- Current client startup automatically hosts/joins after the vanilla title
  screen rather than providing the requested branded public/friends menu.
- Dedicated server development wiring exists, but no separately delivered,
  verified public-server product was established in this audit.
- The current server activates battles for a save named Graves and Glory;
  dedicated-server configuration must account for that requirement.

PROGRESSION TRUST BOUNDARY
Campaign.importProfile accepts a portable client profile when its match count
is at least the stored profile's count. Sanitizing values does not establish
that they were legitimately earned. This is suitable only for the existing
trusted friends-hosted model, not official public progression.

CONTENT/ECONOMY CONSTRAINT
Six troop unlocks cost 600 gold total. Additional daily income would complete
the present collection quickly. Expand the reward/content plan before using
frequent rolls as the main long-term reason to return.

EXISTING TEST EVIDENCE
Local evidence reports passes for collection/combat, team modes, rewards,
barracks, bot replacement, contact feedback and other development checks.
These do not establish public matchmaking, persistent parties, secure economy,
daily reset behavior or a full eight-human Internet match.

DOCUMENTATION WARNING

Reconcile documentation against the final package instead of treating every
