## What I implemented
- `BattleMath` (pure): `travelTicks` rounds distance/speed and clamps it to [min,max]. `tookLoss` is true when the count fell or health fell by at least 0.01 (with a 1e-6 float tolerance).
- `Projectiles`:
  - `Shot` record, plus `fire`, `tick`, `clear`, `inFlight` and `TAG="gravesandglory-shot"`.
  - Arrows are vanilla `ArrowEntity` set to no-gravity, no-clip and DISALLOWED pickup, and tagged. Each tick the server steers the arrow so it meets a moving target on its landing tick (velocity plus `velocityModified`).
  - Bolts and mends have no entity. They show as a team-tinted dust orb (size 1.4), with WITCH particles for Dusk and ENCHANT for Dawn.
  - When a shot lands:
    - arrow: sets `lastHitBy`, deals damage through `mobAttack(shooter.mob)`, then flashes.
    - mend: heals the target and shows hearts.
    - bolt: flashes only.
  - The bolt's landing tick is capped at `windupUntil`.
  - If the target is dead, removed or in another world, the shot is dropped with no damage and its arrow is discarded.
- `Tactics`: ranged non-ability units fire an `arrow` instead of dealing damage at once. The 10-step particle line is removed and the shoot sound kept. Blight start fires a `bolt`, and mend fires a `mend` shot instead of healing at once.
- `BattleEffects`: added `flash(b, at, team)`, which spawns `impactParticles` faction-colour dust particles (size 1.4) plus a 3-particle crit puff and updates `flashes`/`lastFlashColor`. Melee `strike` now calls `flash` at the victim in the attacker's team colour, replacing the old 4-crit puff.
- `GravesAndGlory`:
  - `Unit.lastHitBy` is set in `allowDamage` when the attacker's squad can be resolved, and by `Projectiles` before arrow damage.
  - Added `Battle.shots`.
  - `Projectiles.tick` runs in the running/after-intro block before the unit ticks.
  - `clearOldUnits` also discards shot-tagged entities.
  - `startRound` clears shots.
  - `AFTER_DEATH` falls back to `victim.lastHitBy`.
- `Campaign.returnLobby` and `switchMap` call `Projectiles.clear`.
- `CommandMode`:
  - Added `FLASH_MS=450`, `noteGroups` and `flashing`.
  - The group panel has a 194x3 bar at y+24 (gold fill, danger colour `0xffd9634c` while flashing) and a danger border while flashing.
  - The client STATE receiver calls `noteGroups` before replacing `groups`.
- Sheet `combatDiscipline`: added arrowSpeed 2.2, boltSpeed 1.2, projectileMinTicks 2, projectileMaxTicks 14, impactParticles 8. `Spec.java` and `preflight.json` were regenerated by preflight (real changes, not line-ending churn).
- Harness:
  - `Phase1Checks` provides `report`, `shot`, `server`, `skirmish`, `Suite`, `finish` and the `SUITES` map.
  - Added an optional `ServerWatch` interface: the suite gets a call on the server thread after every `Battle.tick`, for exact per-tick timing.
  - The harness sets `pauseOnLostFocus=false` every client tick, and the test root `options.txt` also contains it.
  - Wired into `isolated-checks.gradle` and `fabric.mod.json`. `CampaignChecks` returns early when `phase1Checks` is set. `release_config.py` forbids `Phase1Checks` and `BattleMathCheck`.

## Fixture notes
- Each check runs in its own controlled fixture:
  - Camera: the player is a spectator 12 blocks to the side and 6 up, looking at the midpoint, so troops ignore the camera.
  - Reinforcement and bot respawn are disabled during the combat fixture.
  - "The volley stops" means every unit is frozen (`nextAttack`/`abilityAt`=MAX).
  - Team colours: first only one Dusk archer may fire, then only one Dawn soldier may strike, so the flash colour is unambiguous.
  - Orphan check: spawns a bonebow and two knights, fires, discards the first target, waits 2 ticks, fires again, then calls `finish(-1)` in the same tick.
- Tick cost: `skirmish(...,4,...)`, then the player's squad is converted to a bot and refilled, giving 8 bot armies and 160 troops. They are placed around the centre point (B on map 0) for 600 ticks.

## Ambiguities resolved
- `skirmish` returns `boolean`: false while the map is switching or building, or the player's squad is missing. Callers retry every tick, since a server task cannot block.
- `difficulty` is accepted but unused, because no bot tiers exist yet.
- A shot whose target dies or is removed is dropped immediately, not at `lands`. The outcome is the same: no damage and the arrow is discarded.
- `lastHitBy` is set only by arrows, never by mend, which targets allies. Mend landing shows hearts with no team flash. Bolt landing does flash.
- Mage and healer attacks on enemies used to draw a flame line toward the enemy, though they deal no damage. That line is gone (now only the strike's magic particles and the cast sound remain).

## Concerns
1. **The brief's HUD fixture value cannot trip its own threshold.** A full vanguard group has 12 knights at 36 max HP each (30 x 1.2). Damaging one knight by 4 drops the group average by 4/36/12 = 0.0093, which is under `tookLoss`'s 0.01, so the first run failed `p1-hud-flash`. I kept `tookLoss` at 0.01 as specified and changed only the test fixture to deal 5 damage (one knight's blow), with a comment in `Phase1Checks`. In real play, a single 4-damage arrow on a full 12-knight group will not flash. The controller may prefer a lower threshold (for example 0.005, which still passes all five standalone checks).
2. **The brief's in-game launch command does not work from this scratchpad as written.** The scratchpad path is too long for Windows CreateProcess (error 267, "directory name is invalid"), and a Git Bash `/c/...` path also fails. I used the folder's 8.3 short path as `GRAVES_ROADMAP_TEST_ROOT`. The data still lives under the scratchpad.
3. The bottom of the HUD's Support panel is covered by chat and advancement lines. This layout was already there before my change and is outside this task's scope.
