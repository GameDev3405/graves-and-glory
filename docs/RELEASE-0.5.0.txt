Graves and Glory 0.5.0 — Houses local candidate
October 23, 2026

Five Houses join the game: the Gilded Order, Graveborn, Wildmoot, Ironclad
and Tidecallers, thirty troops in all, each House with its own trait tiers
and its own way of fighting. Your collection now lives in a schema 2 file:
the ten Commons are yours from the start, rolls draw from the House you pick,
duplicates raise stars, Epics are guaranteed by pity, and Houses pay shards,
Renown and Mastery after matches of three minutes or more. Your old camp
migrates on first load: the original file is kept beside it as <id>.v1.json,
the Sunweaver and Gravecaller (and the Oracle and Wraith, if you had them)
are refunded as 300 gold each, and your class becomes Champion, Warden or
Warlock. No group breaks and runs any more; a line that is losing makes a
LAST STAND. Six commander classes act on the H key. Troops draw from their own
models and animation clips instead of the vanilla mobs' look. The camp has a
recruit page with a tab per House, a troop card and a roll result card.

Not in this build: levels and gear, and painted art for all but the six
Gilded troops. This build ships only after the levels-and-gear plan has run
and every troop has its tier I to III art. Tidecallers and Wildmoot lose most
bot matches and are left for the balance pass.
