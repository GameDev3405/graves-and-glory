# Ironhold layout scan, 2026-11-02

Plan tasks 4A.3 and 3.1 prep. Produced by `python tools/mapforge scan docs/maps/ironhold/manifest.json --world WORLD --design sheets/design.json` (new, read-only: `tools/mapforge/scan.py`). It changes no spawn, capture, route or landmark row.

## What was scanned

No built Ironhold world is committed, so the scan world was assembled offline from two sources, both outside git:

- x -240..240: the full-map adoption batch from the 2026-11-01 spike (`ironhold-adopt.runs.gz`, 18.4 M cells), converted with `adopt`'s `to_world`.
- x below -240 (the Dawn camp shelf and columns A to C): the sections' plans re-applied in review order on the stand-in base world. 19 of 24 sections in columns A to C match the manifest's last passing digest. A0, A2, A5, C3 and C4 do not (their surface plans refuse, because the passes read neighbouring cells); they differ from the batch in 2014 of 468 k overlapping cells. Treat the camp area as approximate. Columns D to K were taken from the batch only.

Walk rules: step up 1, drop 3, two blocks of headroom, water at most 2 deep, diagonals need both sides free. Distances are walking blocks from the spawn node to the node nearest each target.

## Reading it

- Spawns: Dawn (-314, 0) stands at y 73 on the camp shelf with nothing above; Dusk (128, 0) stands at y 64 with nothing above. Both reach every capture ring and Siege site except the two south supply yards.
- Walk to the points: Dawn to B 314 blocks, Dusk to B 128 (D1a made the lanes unequal on purpose).
- Capture rings A, B and C: no blocks inside y 64..67, no water in the band.
- Landmark rows: 25 Ironhold rows, fingerprint above. The game's rows were written for the shipped terrain; about half sit a few blocks off the rebuilt ground. They are replaced in the same change that adopts the map (2A.5), so they were left alone.

## Findings that need a decision (not applied)

1. South supply yards unreachable: ih-supply-sw (-125, 66) and ih-supply-se (140, 66) are 12 by 15 pedestals at y 71 with no step or ramp (neighbours are y 64; 7-high drop). No spawn can walk to them, which fails the house-mode goal "march from the spawn to the yard". Fix is map art (a ramp or stair per pedestal), not a number; it needs a map section edit.
2. Supply rows' elevation: all four rows say 77 and the scanned yards stand at 70 (north yards at 67 and 64 near the rows). Row elevations must be regenerated at adoption.
3. Dusk watchtowers ih-dusk-tower-n/s at (180, +-85) are inside the gorge (ground y 45; playable ground ends near x 165); the layout puts east watchtowers at (150, +-150). Moving them is a layout change.
4. The Dusk camp fan roads in CampaignMaps still run to (+-290, 0) although the Dusk spawn is (128, 0).
5. Lane-head rule (MAP-STANDARDS section 9): Dawn 314 versus Dusk 128 breaks the equal-lane-head rule; the section now records Ironhold as an exception, pending Kort.
6. The x 72 east curtain rows (ih-curtain-en1/en2/es1/es2) stay until adoption (Kort 2026-10-31).
