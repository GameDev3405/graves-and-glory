# Chapter 5 outline: "The Rest" (Graveborn, the Hollow King)

Status: draft for Kort's review, 2026-11-09. Same ground rules as `chapter-1-outline-2026-11-09.md` and `lore-bible-2026-11-09.md`; the approved design wins any disagreement. Calls I made alone are marked **[call]**. No mechanics, rosters or map layouts change.

## What the chapter does

The player commands the Graveborn, rebels from the Skeleton King's army who broke the binding this year. They are distrusted by every House because they were once part of the king's army and because of what they did while bound. The chapter is about owning that, which is why it closes the campaign. It tells the Skeleton King's story (his realm was dying and the confederacy looked away), shows the Graveborn's two wrongs as memories, and ends on the one fight every House must join.

Hints: the binding frays, and the rebellion itself is the hint. Folk myth: the Rest (Ysmorren), the belief that the dead are owed a rest they were denied. The finale ends with the spire's light, the first time the game says the land is alive.

The Hollow King leads, a mocking title the rebels gave their leader, the Skeleton King's defected general. **[call]** The only named character is the Hollow King; the Skeleton King is unnamed and appears in battle 6. Speakers are the Hollow King and a bound soldier (titled).

## Pages

### Page 1: The First Free Night
1. `c5_free` (book, three stills). The dead standing in the dark, thinking for the first time in a century. Caption: "We were bound for a hundred and fifty years. We woke on the same night."
2. `c5_hollow` (card, the camp). The Hollow King, first words: "They call me the Hollow King. They are not wrong. I was hollow a long time."
3. `c5_rest` (card, optional, a bound soldier). The Rest: the dead are owed a rest. **[call]** The soldier does not know why the binding broke.

### Page 2: The First Free Night (battle)
1. `c5_b1_before` (stage, Hollowwood, night). The king's recalled dead rise to rebind the rebels.
2. `battle_1` The First Free Night (Survival, Easy).
3. `c5_b1_after` (stage). A bound soldier's hand stops mid-grip. "It is pulling at me, from somewhere. Not the king. Something older." Hint.

### Page 3: The Barrow Road
1. `c5_b2_before` (stage, Kingsfield). A Gilded patrol shoots on sight. "We are not what you remember."
2. `battle_2` The Barrow Road (Domination, Normal).
3. `c5_b2_after` (stage). Aldric's patrol captain, titled, spits: "You wore his colours." The Hollow King: "Yes."

### Page 4: The King's Story
1. `c5_king` (card, the war table). The Hollow King tells how the Skeleton King came to bind the dead: his realm was dying, the confederacy looked away, he bound the dead to defend the people who were left. "He was not wrong at the start. He was wrong for a century after." **[call]** This is where the player first hears the Skeleton King's motive.
2. `c5_archive_levy` (card item). The Tengervald levy roll, the same one struck from the relief list in chapter 1.

### Page 5: The Razed Dens
1. `c5_b3_before` (stage, Frostmarch). The Hollow King: "I remember this. I do not remember choosing it."
2. `battle_3` The Razed Dens (Domination, Normal; memory).
3. `c5_b3_after` (stage). The Hollow King kneels at a den: "We did this. It was in our hands."

### Page 6: The Sunk Fleet
1. `c5_b4_before` (stage, Rivergate). The Tidecaller ships at anchor.
2. `battle_4` The Sunk Fleet (Domination, Normal; memory).
3. `c5_b4_after` (stage). Water closes over a Tidecaller hull. A bound soldier: "We cannot even say we are sorry in a way that matters."

### Page 7: The Rebinding
1. `c5_b5_before` (stage, Hollowwood). The king's army comes to rebind them. A Wildmoot herd crosses the field uninvited, and the hunter beside them nods once.
2. `battle_5` The Rebinding (Domination, Normal; Wildmoot ally).
3. `c5_b5_after` (stage). The Houses have not forgiven anything; they are standing at the same fire. **[call]** The ring stays open.

### Page 8: The King's Host
1. `c5_b6_before` (stage, Kingsfield, night). The king's army in its whole length; the Skeleton King himself at the head, tired and certain. He says one thing to the Hollow King: "You could have stayed."
2. `battle_6` The King's Host (Domination, five points, Hard; Wildmoot and Tidecaller allies).
3. `c5_b6_after` (stage). The army breaks. The Skeleton King withdraws; if the player's banner strikes him, the Hollow King says "Not yet." **[call]** He lives either way, for seasons 2 and 3. The Houses stand in the field's silence, and none of them touches another.
4. `c5_spire` (book, two stills, unskippable first time). The sky over the capital far to the east: a column of violet-white light rises and holds. Every House turns to look. The Hollow King: "The dead asked to rest. Something has heard." The land under the Houses' feet rises and falls once like a breath. End of season 1.

## Voice directions

The Hollow King: dry, tired, honest. A bound soldier: flat, slow. The Skeleton King: gentle, certain, kind, which is the horror. Aldric's patrol captain: bitter.

## What this chapter leaves for others

- Season 2: the Skeleton King keeps hunting the Houses; the giants arrive and Orruvan wakes under the capital.
- The rebels' ownership of what they did is the seed of the Graveborn's season 3 turn.
