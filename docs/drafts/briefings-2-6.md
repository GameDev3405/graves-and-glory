## Battle 2: The Moon Shrine (Hollowwood Reach, Survival, Easy)
- Current: The old graves of Hollowwood are opening. Hold the shrine until the last of the dead lies still.
- Alternate: The graves of Hollowwood are opening under the moon. Hold the shrine and keep your banner alive until dawn.
- Lesson: holding ground against waves, spending troops sparingly (bonus: lose at most 8).

## Battle 3: Ironhold Retaken (Ironhold Siege, Siege attack, Normal)
- Current: The dead took Ironhold while its smiths slept. The Ironclad bring the engines; you bring the swords. Take the yard, break the gate, seize the keep.
- Alternate: The dead hold Ironhold and its gate stands shut. Take the yard, then let the engines open the way to the keep.
- Lesson: staged objectives and working with allied siege engines (bonus: gate inside six minutes).

## Battle 4: Raiders on the River (Rivergate Valley, Banner, Normal)
- Current: Tidecaller raiders have come up the river for the march's banner. Guard yours and carry theirs home.
- Alternate: Tidecaller raiders have come for the march's banner. Guard yours at the bridge and bring theirs back across.
- Lesson: split roles, one group guards while another raids (bonus: carry their banner yourself).

## Battle 5: The Frozen Pass (Frostmarch Pass, Domination, Normal)
- Current: The Wildmoot let no host through Frostmarch that has not bled for it. Take the pass before the snow closes it.
- Alternate: The Wildmoot hold Frostmarch and the snow is coming. Take the cairns and keep them until the pass is yours.
- Lesson: tempo on a hold-points map (bonus: win inside ten minutes). I avoided a cairn count; check the map's point names before using any.

## Battle 6: Kingsfield (Kingsfield Crossroads, five points, Hard)
- Current: The Hollow King's host has reached Kingsfield. The Houses you met stand with you now. Break his host and the road is yours.
- Alternate: Every road meets at Kingsfield, and the Hollow King waits where they cross. Lead the Houses you have met and break his host.
- Lesson: everything at once, with allies (bonus: strike down the King with your own banner).
