# Sword-and-shield motion references (C4 / R99)

A link list, not a gallery. No images are stored here: downloading files needs Kort's permission, which this task did not
have, and most of these sources are copyrighted. Open the links to look; do not copy the images into the repo without a
licence check. The MS I.33 page was opened in this session (2026-11-01) to confirm it; the others were written from known
stable pages and not opened, so check one before quoting it.

Use them for timing and silhouette only. Our art rules still win: 1 texel per pixel, no flags, pennants or back standards.

## How each was used in this task

- **Guard, wall, brace (R98):** the I.33 and Meyer plates bend the knees well past 30 degrees and keep the shield elbow bent;
  ours now bend the knees 36-65 degrees and the shield elbow 30-32 degrees, with the board's facing unchanged.
- **Thrust (K3/R97/M4):** I.33 keeps the buckler in front of the sword hand through the thrust; ours keeps the board within
  30 degrees of forward (the guard's own) through the thrust, instead of turning it 76 degrees edge-on.
- **Deaths (K2):** the tapestry's dead lie bent and grounded; ours lie on the ground with elbows and knees at 30 degrees or
  more, and each kind has a second variant (a drop to the knees, or a different fall).
