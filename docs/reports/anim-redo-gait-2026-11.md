# Anim redo gait: handoff (2026-11-05)

Branch `art/anim-redo-gait` (from `art/anim-redo` at caef2220), checkout `bannerbound-animredo-gait`. Not pushed or merged.

The procedural biped walk and run move toward the Fresh Animations style on rigs that have the waist and ankle bones:

- the chest turns against the hips;
- the hips bob, with the weight landing on contact;
- the feet strike with the heel and push off with the toe;
- the arms swing from the chest, and the forearms trail;
- the run leans further and takes a longer stride.

Movement speed, hitboxes, gameplay values, clip ids, lengths, impact ticks and design.json stats are unchanged. Spec.java is
not hand-edited; it is regenerated by verify and restored before commit.

## What changed

### Runtime (src/main/java)

**TroopMotion** gates the new stride. `waist(m)` is true when a rig has `chest`, `right_foot` and `left_foot`, and it sets
`state.waist` in both `pose` and `simulate`.

- ic_colossus, tc_brute, wm_oldhorn, wm_hound and wm_stalker lack those bones and keep the old stride, bit for bit.
- The check below asserts that the stride writes no chest or foot channel when waist is off.

**MotionBody.strideWaist** is the waist stride. The constants are in the class.

| Feature | How it works | Constants |
|---|---|---|
| Counter-rotation | The hips (root) twist with the stride. body and chest turn back further, so the chest's world yaw runs against the hips. The head is held steadier. | TWIST_WALK 6, TWIST_RUN 9, BODY_TWIST 0.35, CHEST_TWIST 1.45, HEAD_STEADY 0.85 |
| Bob | Two dips per cycle. A landing pulse comes in the first LAND_SPAN of each stance. | BOB_WALK 0.6, BOB_RUN 0.3, PULSE_WALK 1.5, PULSE_RUN 2.5, LAND_SPAN 0.24 |
| Weight shift | The hips shift and roll over the stance foot, and the chest rolls against them. | SHIFT_WALK 0.35, HIP_ROLL 1.2, CHEST_ROLL 2.0 |
| Lean | The lean comes from the hips and chest, not from the head. | LEAN_WALK 3, LEAN_RUN 13 |
| Arms | The arms swing from the chest. The forearm trails the arm by ARM_LAG of a cycle, flexes more on the back swing, and keeps ELBOW_MIN of bend. | ARM_WALK 26, ARM_RUN 16, FOREARM_FLEX 14, ARM_LAG 0.08, ARM_FOLLOW 0.55, ELBOW_MIN 3 |
| Run stride | The run's half-stride is longer at the waist. | HALF_RUN_WAIST 7.8 |

**MotionFeet.strideAnkle** handles the foot during each phase of the step:

- **Swing:** the foot hangs toe-down after toe-off (HANG_WALK 8 / HANG_RUN 12 to HANG_U). It then pre-sets toe-up for the strike
  (STRIKE_WALK -14 / STRIKE_RUN -6).
- **Contact and stance:** the foot lands on the heel and rolls flat by FLAT_AT 0.2, pivoting on the heel. It then rolls onto the
  toe (TOE_WALK 28 / TOE_RUN 35), pivoting on the toe.
- **Ankle target:** the ankle target is solved so that the pivot point stays on the ground. The knee then follows it.
- **Clamping:** the pitch is clamped to ±FOOT_MAX 40, net of the clip's own pitch (`clampFoot`). The yaw is clamped to FOOT_YAW 15.

Two rounds of fixes went into stopping:

1. **Round 1:**
   - The settling step rolls the foot flat (ROLL_V 1.2, ROLL_T 0.12, with `rollW` easing).
   - A landing keeps the swing's pitch (`footKf`).
   - A foot's pivot only moves one way and lands on its lower end.
2. **Round 2:**
   - `footPitch` returns the wanted, unclamped pitch, so the ankle target puts the ground points under the pitch the foot
     wants.
   - Only the drawn pitch is clamped. This removed the slide caused by the clamp's error fed back into the ankle.

**MotionEngine** adds the per-foot State fields (pitch, pivot, solve, roll and landing memory) and `waist`. It also adds the
Frame fields `hipYaw` and `travelF`.

### Shared tools

- **test-tools/java/.../MotionGrade.java:**
  - `GRAVES_GRADE_LOCO_ANGLES` (for example `0,45,90`) shoots each moving loco segment once per camera angle round the troop's
    facing. A segment shot at an angle other than 90 is named `NAME-aANGLE`.
  - The marks and crops use that camera yaw.
  - Unset, it does what it did before: 90 only, the side view.
- **test-tools/standalone/MotionEngineCheck.java:**
  - The rig is foot-aware.
  - `groundRun` grounds on the foot corners.
  - New checks: `waist gait` and `stop settle` (see below).

## Open issues

Recorded rather than fixed: the stop and stride issues used their 2 fix rounds.

1. **Stop crouch:** after some stop phases, the feet end about 16 px apart, heel to heel. The back foot sits at the -40° clamp
   with its heel up, and the hips dip up to 6 px (p90 3.6). The cause is the longer stride plus the toe pivot. Possible fixes:
   - a shorter HALF_RUN_WAIST;
   - a settle step that closes the stance.
2. **Archer run overstretch:** the run's 90° view shows a near-split at frame 10 and a deep crouch at frames 1 and 7. The torso
   is low and upright. Possible fix: lower HALF_RUN_WAIST or the pelvis drop for the bow family, or for all troops.
3. **Mage walk splay:** in the walk's 90° view, frames 1-2 and 7-8 show an A-shaped splay with a drop. Under the robe, the heel
   and toe cannot be read. Possible fix: a robe-specific stride scale.
4. **Mage skate:** it cannot be measured, because the harness reads the robe and not the feet.
5. **Archer and tc_mist outliers:** the largest single plant on some segments is 0.33-0.56 blocks. These come from the run
   start's first step and from descending landings, not from steady slip (see above).
6. **Pitch clamp:** the foot pitch hits the clamp on 21.6% of frames at 2.4 b/s, which predates the stop fixes. At 3.4 b/s it is
   0.3%, and at 6.1 b/s it is 8.3%. A backpedal landing is also clamped.
7. **Forearm lag:** it is 1 frame, the check's minimum. It reads weakly on screen.
8. **Bob:** it reads weakly on most troops. The contact sink is 0.48 px on the walk and 0.85 px on the run.
9. **Run knee:** the run stance's knee sits at 65-85°.
10. **HELD checks:** `run start` and `hit over attack window` were HELD before this branch, and still are.
