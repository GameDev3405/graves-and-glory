# Economy model wired to the sheet (plan 5.4)

`tools/economy_model.py` now reads its one-off gold from `sheets/design.json` instead of the hand-set `economyModel.chapterGold` (300) and `chapterRareValue` (300). Both keys are removed from the sheet row and from `ECONOMY_INPUTS` in `tools/preflight.py`.

- Chapter gold = chapter 1 battles x (`campaign.completionGold` 80 + `victoryGold` 40, as Campaign.java pays a win) = 720, plus the `bonusObjectives` gold of each battle's bonus, all assumed earned: b1 40, b2 40, b3 40, b4 40 (placeholder row), b5 50, b6 60 = 270. Total 990.
- Tier-20 Rare value = `rarities.rare.chosenPrice` = 300 (unchanged).
- Still on the model row: matchGold, winGold and the contract and pace inputs (no separate sheet source). The band is now 29 h +/- 12 percent (25.5 to 32.5 h), replacing the old 25 to 40 h check.

Run: seed 4909, 20,000 runs, income 492 gold an hour.

| | Before (300 chapter gold) | After (990) |
|---|---|---|
| Median hours | 28.7 | 27.3 |
| P10 to P90 hours | 21.1 to 35.8 | 19.7 to 34.4 |
| Median gold needed | 14,100 | 13,400 |

The gold distribution (median 14,700, P10 11,000, P90 18,200) is unchanged. The median is inside the band (25.5 to 32.5 h); the sheet was not retuned. If the bonuses are not all earned (wins only, 720) the median is about 27.8 h; it stays in the band either way. The b1 bonus was included although the task list named b2 to b6, because the sheet gives it 40 gold; without it the median is about 27.3 h. b4 is still a placeholder objective.

Test: `test-tools/standalone/test_economy_model.py`.
