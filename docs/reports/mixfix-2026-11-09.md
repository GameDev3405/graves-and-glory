## 2. How the script decides

Rules live at the top of `mixfix.py` (constants and two small tables). Summary:

1. **Peak.** A file whose 4x oversampled peak is above -1.05 dBTP (or ebur128 true peak above -1.0) is brought to about -1.2 dBTP. Gain down first. The limiter (ffmpeg `alimiter`, attack 5 ms, release 50 ms, auto-level off) is used only if gain alone would take the file more than 2 LU below where it should be. The result must pass the peak gate after the Vorbis encode, so the script re-renders from the existing file until it does.
2. **Variant groups.** Fixed target per group (the audit median, adjusted where headroom stops a quiet take rising; see `GROUP_TARGETS`). A take more than 0.7 LU from the target is moved by gain to within 0.4 LU of it, max 6 dB. A raise never passes the peak ceiling. A group therefore ends within 1.4 LU.
3. **File rules.** `ui_back_7` and the four rarity files have a fixed level each (`FILE_TARGETS`).
4. Everything else is left alone. Files under 0.4 s cannot be gated for loudness: they get peak fixes only.

## 5. What I could not fully fix, and why

- **Short files (12 under 0.4 s)** have no integrated loudness. `dummy_thud_1`, `dummy_thud_2` and `page_turn_1` were hot and are fixed by gain (peak only). The groups `banner_drag`, `dummy_thud` (3 takes), `convoy_stop`, `clock_tick`, `page_turn` cannot have their spread checked or matched with this method; a short-term or RMS measure would be needed.
- **`relic_use_ice_1` and `sword_clash_1`** came down 4.9 and 3.6 dB. The two relic ice takes were clipped at the source, and Vorbis re-encodes a flat-topped transient with a few dB of overshoot, so more gain than the nominal 3 dB was needed. This is gain only (no limiter). Ice is now about -19 LUFS (was -15 to -16).
- **`sword_clash_4`** is untouched and sets the group's floor (-19.7), so the other three takes were brought down to about -18.8, by 1.8 to 3.6 dB. The clashes are the commonest combat sound and are now quieter than before; if they should be louder, the take 4 headroom (-1.9 dBTP) is the limit without a limiter on every take.
- **`rarity_rare_2`** has no headroom (-1.1 dBTP) so the ladder is built around it, not up to the epic level a louder ladder would use. The whole ladder sits at -25.4 to -22.3 effective; common came up 2.6 LU, uncommon down 2.9.
- **Peak margin is thin on a few files** (true peak -1.0 to -1.1 after the encode): `ui_back_7`, `rarity_rare_2`, `page_turn_1`, `coin_clink_5` (untouched at -1.1 by the 4x check). All at or below -1.0 dBTP by the audit's measurement. Raise the margin in `CEILING_DBTP` if a later step wants -1.5.
- The five music files named in the task were only measured, not touched (they were already within -1 dBTP). `title_music_3` and `_6` were moved 0.4 to 0.5 dB to bring their spread from 1.7 to 0.9; they are unplayed.
