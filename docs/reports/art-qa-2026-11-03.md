## Findings
- **F1 (low, data).** `sheets/design.json` `pennant` is still `true` for all 30 troops; Kort's 2026-10-30 ruling was to turn the column off. No model has a pennant or standard bone, so nothing draws on any back today (TroopModel only draws bones with part `pennant`/`standard` when the row flies one). Housekeeping, not a visible defect; not touched (generated Spec.java).
- **F2 (medium, House colour).** Graveborn Stalker is icy cyan (peak hue 194, 74 to 81% inside the Tidecallers sea-blue band); Bonebow s1/s2 peak hue 80 (olive green). Neither reads as violet. Confirmed on the skin sheet.
- **F3 (medium, House colour, tier III).** Graveborn tier III skins on Revenant, Bonebow, Reaper, Hexer and Hollow King carry large gold/brass blocks: violet band share 11 to 18% with 66 to 87% in the gold band, against 22 to 80% at tiers I/II. Violet is present only as thin trim. Check whether gold trim at tier III is intended for Graveborn.
- **F4 (low to medium, House colour).** Tidecallers Harpooner (peak 35) and Tideguard (peak 30) are brown-dominant at tiers I/II; the sea-blue share (32 to 50%) passes the 10% rule but the peak is not blue. Raider, Mist, Brute and Admiral peak at 208 to 213.
- **Caveats.** The hue tool's bands overlap on warm hues (leather and skin vs gold, ochre, ember), so F3/F4 are leads that need a visual read in game; the hue metric and its 10% pass are the tool's choice, as on 11-02.
