## Follow-up rulings (Kort, 2026-11-02, branch `work/qa-rules`)
- I7/I9: a stopped or crashed match pays the participation gold pro-rated by the share of the round played (rounded down), no win bonus, shards, Renown, XP or record. A stop pays at once; a crash pays on the next start from a checkpoint written every 30 s, so up to 30 s of play is lost. Code: `MatchRules.partialGold`, `Campaign.voidMatch`, `checkpoint`, `payInterrupted`.
- J3: `/warp` is ops only; the code already required level 2.
- H8: a dropped player's slot is held 2 minutes (`MatchRules.HOLD_SECONDS`), then the reservation is released and the army stays with its bot as an ordinary seat. The 60 s bot takeover is unchanged.
- Iris and Sodium notices and `iris-source.zip` stay in the server kit. No change.
