Graves and Glory 0.4.0 — solo rework local candidate
October 18, 2026

Arrows, bolts and spells now fly before they land, with team-coloured puffs
where they hit, and a group card flashes when its troops take hits. A group
that is shattered or taken from behind breaks and runs for home with a horn,
then rallies; an order given during the rout applies when it regroups.
Recruits trained at the barracks march out from a point your side has held
for ten seconds, near the fight, instead of the base. Bots come in Easy,
Normal and Hard: Hard decides faster, braces a shieldwall against archers and
charges only from close range. The first battle of The Long March, The
Training Field, now teaches command with four lessons that name your own
keys. Every match ends with a battle report: kills, losses, gold and time on
points for each commander, with an MVP. The camp snapshot no longer carries
match ids to the client, so a long-lived profile cannot outgrow the packet.

Verified by automated checks in an isolated development client. Bot tier
balance is measured and parked until the Phase 3 maps. This candidate has not
been installed into a personal launcher, published, or played by Kort yet.
